## KEY INSIGHT: THE FULL-SERVICE BUNDLE IS THE MOAT

Every competitor in Vienna sells ONE thing: a website OR social media OR video OR IT support. Nobody sells all four as a coordinated package. That's c2moviez × exeoflow's structural advantage.

**The pitch that unlocks everything:**
> "Most agencies will sell you a website. We build the whole digital presence — the website, the content that fills it, the social that drives traffic to it, and the IT infrastructure that keeps it secure. One relationship, one point of accountability."

When Claudio says this in a room, no competitor can match it.
