# ExeoFlow x c2moviez --- Contract Framework

> **Purpose:** Define a fair, enforceable partnership agreement between c2moviez GmbH and ExeoFlow (Raphael Fuchs) that reflects the significant investment already made and secures c2moviez's position as the exclusive creative partner going forward.

---

## Background

c2moviez has been ExeoFlow's creative and go-to-market partner since inception. This is not a new vendor relationship --- Claudio was there from day one, when Raphael first started building ExeoFlow. The partnership was built on a handshake agreement to push ExeoFlow together.

**What c2moviez has delivered:**

- **65-70+ hours** of direct work by CTI across marketing strategy, text animation, SaaS explainer videos, motion graphics, and video production
- Full go-to-market strategy development (content, branding, sales positioning, DACH market approach)
- Marketing content pipeline: demo videos, product feature visualisations, social media assets
- Creative team assembled and coordinated: CTI (strategy, production, client acquisition), Silas Wirth (motion graphics, text animation, SaaS explainers), Marcel/NexusLink (video post-production, editing)
- Integration of ExeoFlow into c2moviez internal operations (Plane.so sync, ops.c2moviez.com dashboard)

**The problem:** Raphael is now entering contract discussions with third parties to sell and distribute ExeoFlow, without a definitive agreement in place with c2moviez. This risks diluting the partnership and leaving c2moviez's substantial contribution uncompensated. A binding contract is now required.

## Proposed Deal Structure

### Option A: Revenue Share (Recommended)

The cleanest model for a founding creative partner. c2moviez shares in ExeoFlow's success proportionally.

| Term | Proposal |
|------|----------|
| **Revenue share** | 15-20% of ExeoFlow net subscription revenue |
| **Scope covered** | All marketing, video, motion graphics, SaaS content, sales support |
| **Minimum term** | 24 months, auto-renewal in 12-month periods |
| **Payment** | Monthly, within 30 days of month-end |
| **Reporting** | Full CRM access + monthly revenue statement |
| **Exclusivity** | c2moviez is the exclusive creative/marketing partner for ExeoFlow |
| **Right of first refusal** | All creative, video, and marketing work goes to c2moviez first |
| **Tail period** | Revenue share continues 12 months after termination on existing clients |
| **Retroactive** | CHF 9,100-9,800 credited or paid per settlement terms above |

**Why this works:** Aligns incentives. c2moviez earns more when ExeoFlow grows. Raphael gets a committed creative team without fixed overhead. Both parties win.

---

### Option B: Retainer + Commission

Guaranteed income for c2moviez with upside on client acquisition.

| Term | Proposal |
|------|----------|
| **Monthly retainer** | CHF 3,000-5,000 for guaranteed creative capacity |
| **Commission** | 10-15% per client acquired through c2moviez marketing/sales |
| **Commission duration** | Lifetime of referred client's subscription |
| **Scope per month** | Defined deliverables (e.g., X videos, X social assets, X campaign updates) |
| **Payment** | Retainer on 1st of month; commission monthly, net 30 |
| **Minimum term** | 12 months, 90-day termination notice |
| **Exclusivity** | c2moviez is the exclusive creative partner |
| **Retroactive** | CHF 9,100-9,800 paid within 60 days of signing |

**Why this works:** Provides c2moviez with financial predictability. Commission incentivises active client acquisition. Clear deliverables reduce ambiguity.

---

### Option C: Project-Based + Equity

Standard rates for work performed, plus an ownership stake recognising the founding contribution.

| Term | Proposal |
|------|----------|
| **Hourly rate** | CHF 140/h (standard c2moviez rate) |
| **Project pricing** | Quoted per project at standard rates |
| **Equity stake** | 5-10% in ExeoFlow for founding creative contribution |
| **Vesting** | Over 24-36 months with 6-month cliff |
| **Payment** | Project invoices net 30 days |
| **Exclusivity** | c2moviez is preferred creative partner (not exclusive) |
| **Retroactive** | 65-70 hours converted to equity value, or paid at CHF 140/h |

**Why this works:** Gives c2moviez skin in the game. Raphael pays market rates for work but shares long-term upside. Equity vesting keeps both parties committed.

## Key Terms (All Options)

### Exclusivity
- c2moviez is the **exclusive creative and marketing partner** for ExeoFlow
- No third-party agencies, freelancers, or partners may produce marketing, video, motion graphics, or sales content for ExeoFlow without c2moviez's written consent
- Raphael retains the right to handle product development, engineering, and direct sales independently
- Any third-party creative engagement requires c2moviez right of first refusal (14-day response window)

### Intellectual Property
- ExeoFlow owns all final deliverables produced under the agreement
- c2moviez retains **portfolio rights** --- the right to showcase work in portfolios, case studies, and marketing materials
- **"Produced by c2moviez"** credit on all video content (demo videos, explainers, social videos, training content)
- c2moviez retains ownership of tools, templates, and processes developed internally

### Termination
- **Notice period:** 90 days written notice
- **Tail period:** Commission / revenue share continues 12 months post-termination on clients acquired during the partnership
- **Retroactive compensation:** Any unpaid retroactive amount (CHF 9,100-9,800+) becomes immediately due upon termination
- **IP post-termination:** ExeoFlow may continue using delivered content; c2moviez retains portfolio rights indefinitely

### Confidentiality
- 24-month NDA survival after termination
- Covers: pricing, client data, revenue figures, product roadmap, business strategy
- Standard exceptions: public domain, independently developed, legally required disclosure

### Governing Law
- Swiss law applies
- Dispute resolution: negotiation (30 days) then mediation then arbitration under Swiss rules

## Questions for Raphael

These must be answered before finalising the contract:

1. **Revenue model:** What pricing model is ExeoFlow using? (monthly subscription, per-seat, tiered plans, custom enterprise pricing?)
2. **Current financials:** What is ExeoFlow's current ARR / MRR? How many paying clients?
3. **Pipeline:** How many prospects are in the sales pipeline? What's the conversion rate?
4. **Growth timeline:** What's the timeline for the next growth phase or funding round?
5. **Exclusivity stance:** Is Raphael open to c2moviez being the exclusive creative partner? If not, why?
6. **Third-party contracts:** What contracts has Raphael already signed or is negotiating with other parties? Do any overlap with c2moviez's scope?
7. **Retroactive recognition:** Does Raphael acknowledge the 65-70+ hours of work already invested? Is he willing to compensate retroactively?
8. **Preferred deal structure:** Which option (A, B, or C) does Raphael prefer? Is he open to a hybrid?
9. **ExeoFlow legal entity:** What is the legal structure of ExeoFlow? (GmbH, sole proprietorship, AG?)
10. **Equity possibility:** If Option C, is Raphael open to a minority equity stake for c2moviez?

## Negotiation Position

**c2moviez's leverage:**
- Only creative team that has worked on ExeoFlow --- all existing marketing assets were produced by c2moviez
- Deep product knowledge from months of hands-on work
- Assembled team (CTI + Silas + Marcel) with complementary skills covering the full production pipeline
- 65-70+ hours of unpaid/underpaid work already delivered in good faith
- DACH market access and client network

**Non-negotiables:**
- Retroactive compensation for past work (CHF 9,100-9,800+ minimum)
- Exclusivity clause for creative/marketing work
- Right of first refusal on all creative engagements
- Portfolio rights and production credit
- Transparent revenue/client reporting

**Acceptable trade-offs:**
- Flexible on exact percentage (revenue share or commission) --- within reason
- Open to hybrid structures combining elements of Options A, B, and C
- Willing to phase in terms (e.g., lower retainer initially, scaling with ExeoFlow growth)
- Vesting terms on equity are negotiable
