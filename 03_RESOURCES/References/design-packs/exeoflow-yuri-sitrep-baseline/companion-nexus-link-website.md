## Why It Is The Better Benchmark

- The current Nexus Link site already has the correct consumer-facing reading order.
- The section structure is calmer and easier to follow than the SITREP baseline.
- The navigation, hero, and CTA rhythm are more production-ready.
- The site already carries stronger visual polish and a more complete brand language.
- It is the right reference for future upgrades in motion, spacing, and information packaging.

## What To Reuse

- Scroll-snap pacing and section sequencing.
- The hero-to-services-to-work flow.
- The sticky navigation treatment.
- The card language and CTA behavior.
- The professional tone of the copy.
- The stronger use of brand systems and layout consistency.

## What To Improve Further

- Make the page feel even less static.
- Add more deliberate animation choreography.
- Reduce any remaining generic template feeling.
- Push the visual language beyond clean layout into stronger editorial presence.

## Use Rule

If ExeoFlow or YURI OS work starts to feel overfilled or overly technical, compare it against this benchmark first. The Nexus Link site is the baseline for presentation quality, not the target-intelligence SITREP artifact.
