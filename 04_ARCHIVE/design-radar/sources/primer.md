# Primer

Source: https://primer.style/design/ and https://primer.github.io/design/

## Signals

- Cohesive, accessible, efficient product UI.
- Strong foundations, patterns, and components.
- Documentation that doubles as a design language.

## Useful patterns

- Efficient tables, filters, and stateful components.
- Accessible defaults.
- Product UI that stays clear under load.

## Apply to NUDIMMUD

- Use Primer as a guide for the operational surfaces.
- Make the project board, discovery catalog, and inspectors feel like real tools.
- Favor obvious interaction states over decorative complexity.
