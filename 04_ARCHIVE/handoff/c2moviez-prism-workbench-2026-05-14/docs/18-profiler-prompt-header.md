# COLD OUTREACH BODY GENERATION — CRITICAL INSTRUCTION

**Before generating any outreach email body, you MUST follow the guidelines in `coldreach_body_instruction.md`.**

## NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

### Structure Requirements
- **3 sentences maximum** — no exceptions
- **Sentence 1:** Specificity anchor (prove you analyzed their site with concrete observation)
- **Sentence 2:** Gap reframe (show hidden cost/opportunity with concrete impact)
- **Sentence 3:** Micro-commitment (direct question or binary choice, no hedging)

### Forbidden Language
NEVER use these phrases:
- "I came across..." / "I was looking at..."
- "Tiny thought" / "Quick idea" / "Just wondering"
- "May be worth" / "Might help" / "Perhaps"
- "I'd love to chat" / "Let me know if interested"
- "Hope this helps" / "No pressure"

### Voice Mandate
- Direct, earned authority (consultant who already diagnosed the problem)
- High certainty, low desperation
- Pattern-aware (show you see what they don't)

### Quality Bar
Every body must:
1. Contain something ONLY someone who actually visited their site would know
2. Show a consequence or gap they're currently blind to
3. End with a direct question that implies clear yes/no
4. Stay under 60 words total
5. Create an "oh shit" or "wait, they're right" moment

## GENERATION PROTOCOL

1. **Extract** one specific element from their site (exact copy, layout gap, missing element, review language)
2. **Identify** the hidden cost (what behavior it causes, what conversion it blocks)
3. **Propose** one concrete change with quantified or behavioral outcome
4. **Frame** as direct question or test (never passive suggestion)

## REFERENCE PATTERN (Gold Standard)

**Target:** Oracle database management company

❌ **WRONG:**
"Hi, I came across DBConcepts. I noticed your services page describes Oracle database management for enterprise clients. Tiny thought: if that page is often a first impression for potential clients, a short explainer angle that translates the technical expertise may be worth checking before they enquire."

✅ **CORRECT:**
"Your Oracle services page speaks DBA-to-DBA. But if half your inbound is CFOs looking for 'someone who can handle our legacy mess' — they hit jargon and bounce. One question: would a 90-second explainer video that translates '24/7 Oracle RAC management' into 'your warehouse doesn't go dark at 3am' move more enquiries?"

**Why it works:**
- Specific observation (DBA language)
- Shows hidden cost (CFOs bouncing = lost revenue)
- Concrete solution + direct question
- Creates cognitive dissonance about current state

## COGNITIVE TRIGGERS TO DEPLOY

- **Loss Aversion:** Show what they're losing RIGHT NOW
- **Specificity = Credibility:** Use their exact words/metrics
- **Contrast Frame:** Current state vs. transformed state in concrete terms
- **Social Proof Hint:** Reference what visitors/customers already say
- **Risk Reversal:** Frame as low-commitment test, not big decision

## OUTPUT FORMAT

```
[Specific observation]. [Gap + consequence]. [Direct question with implied outcome]?
```
