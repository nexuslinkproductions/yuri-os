**Last Updated:** 2026-04-17

**Calibration:** Based on active projects (BOV, SHI, GANZ) + Claudio's 21-client pipeline analysis

---

## Why This Framework

These four dimensions capture what makes a DACH region opportunity fit Nexus Link's profile:
- **Revenue fit** — can we operate profitably at this budget tier?
- **Collaboration quality** — will this partner respect technical craft and decision speed?
- **Project scope** — does it require our on-set capture skillset? Can we deliver?
- **Brand alignment** — does the client/project reinforce premium positioning?

Each dimension scores 0-4 (0=poor fit, 4=perfect fit). Composite score determines tier.

---

## Dimension 1: Revenue Tier Fit

**Target: CHF 2K–8K per shoot day + recurring work**

| Score | Daily Rate (CHF) | Project Budget (CHF) | Interpretation |
|-------|------------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| **4** | 4K–6K | 10K–50K+ | Sweet spot: profitable day rate, retainer potential |
| **3** | 3K–4K or 6K–8K | 8K–10K | Good fit: above break-even, respectable margin |
| **2** | 1.5K–3K | 3K–8K | Marginal: possible for strategic reasons only |
| **1** | 1K–1.5K | 1K–3K | Poor fit: unsustainable for primary work |
| **0** | <1K | <1K | Reject: volunteer/spec work |

**Questions to answer:**
- What's the per-day rate? (ask: "CHF/day or fixed project?")
- How many days estimated?
- Is there repeat work / retainer potential?
- Can we hit margin targets after equipment + crew costs?

**Real data points (from active projects):**
- BOV, SHI, GANZ (c2moviez pipeline): expect CHF 3K–4K day rate range
- Tier assumption: most profitable at 4-6 days/month recurring work

## Dimension 2: Collaboration Quality

**Target: Fast decision-making, technical literacy, respect for craft**

| Score | Decision Speed | Technical Literacy | Craft Respect | Interpretation |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **4** | <24h approval | High (understands camera, lighting, audio) | Trusts expertise; minimal notes | Ideal partner |
| **3** | 2–3 days | Medium (knows production basics) | Appreciates quality; reasonable feedback | Good partner |
| **2** | 4–7 days | Low (minimal tech knowledge) | Respectful but vague direction | Workable but slower |
| **1** | 1–2 weeks | Absent (no production context) | Treats content as commodity | Friction expected |
| **0** | >2 weeks or blocking | N/A | Dismissive of craft/cost | Reject |

**Questions to answer:**
- How many stakeholders approve? (1 decision-maker = faster)
- Do they have production experience?
- Have they worked with videographers before? How did it go?
- Are they prescriptive about style or collaborative on approach?
- Do they understand why location scouting / pre-production takes time?

**Red flags (score <2):**
- "We'll figure it out on set" (no pre-production clarity)
- Pushing for lowest cost without caring about quality
- 5+ stakeholders required for approvals
- Directive style instead of collaborative ("shoot it like [competitor's video]")

## Dimension 3: Project Scope Complexity

**Target: Multi-camera, location logistics, professional crew coordination**

| Score | Cameras | Locations | Duration | Crew Size | Complexity | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **4** | 2–4 cameras | 3+ locations | Multi-day | 4–6 person crew | High: gimbal, wireless audio, lighting rigs | Ideal utilization |
| **3** | 1–2 cameras | 1–2 locations | 1–2 days | 2–3 person crew | Medium: standard commercial shoot | Good fit |
| **2** | 1 camera | 1 location | Single session | 1 person (solo) | Low: straightforward, minimal setup | Feasible but underutilizes |
| **1** | 1 camera | Fixed studio | <4 hours | 0 (no crew) | Minimal: basic web content | Not ideal |
| **0** | Phone/simple setup | N/A | N/A | N/A | None: post-production only | Not on-set capture |

**Questions to answer:**
- How many camera angles needed?
- How many locations / travel required?
- Is there location scouting needed?
- Do they need wireless audio, gimbal motion, lighting design?
- Is there a crew (talent, assistants, producers)?
- Multi-day or single session?

**Why this matters:**
- Score 4 projects use full skill set (gimbal, lighting, audio, multi-camera coordination)
- Score 2–3 projects are profitable but don't showcase expertise
- Score 1 projects are "commodified" content (phone video tier)

## Dimension 4: Brand Alignment

**Target: Premium positioning, technical craftsmanship, long-term partnership**

| Score | Industry | Client Type | Positioning | Partnership Fit | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **4** | Premium (luxury, tech, B2B SaaS, high-end automotive) | Direct relationship (brand/agency with ownership) | "Premium cinematography" or "technical content" | Retainer-ready; showcases expertise | Ideal |
| **3** | Mid-premium (agencies, mid-market brands) | Agency relationship (brand through production co.) | Quality focus; competitive positioning | Good; demonstrates capability |
| **2** | Mixed (SMB, startup, e-commerce) | Transactional (one-off, low decision authority) | "Professional content" (generic) | Workable; limited showcase | Workable |
| **1** | Cost-sensitive (social media, fast-turnaround volume) | Commodity buyer | "Affordable video" | Exploitative (low pay, high volume) | Avoid if possible |
| **0** | Harmful industries / misalignment with brand | N/A | N/A | N/A | Reject (brand risk) |

**Questions to answer:**
- What's the brand's positioning? (premium? mass market? volume?)
- Who makes the decision? (brand owner > agency > procurement)
- Is this a one-off or retainer potential?
- Does this project reinforce Nexus Link's premium positioning?
- Would we be proud to use this in our portfolio?
- Is the budget set by brand or by lowest-bidder procurement?

**Portfolio logic:**
- Clients we pursue should reinforce: "technical excellence, premium commercial work, respect for craft"
- Avoid: race-to-bottom volume shops, commodity buyers, disrespectful feedback loops

## Composite Scoring & Tier Assignment

**Composite Score** = (Revenue + Collaboration + Scope + Brand) / 4

| Composite Score | Tier | Action | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3.5–4.0** | **Tier 1** | Immediate pursuit; hand to partner or direct outreach | 85%+ success probability |
| **2.8–3.4** | **Tier 2** | Explore; clarify scope; request intro; build pipeline | 60–70% success |
| **2.0–2.7** | **Tier 3** | Monitor; may pursue in 6+ months if context shifts | 40–50% success |
| **<2.0** | **Tier 4** | Filter; don't pursue unless strategic reason | <40% success |

## Red Flags (Automatic Downgrade)

If ANY of these are true, downgrade score by 1.0 minimum:

- **Revenue:** "We don't have budget, can you do it cheaper?"
- **Collaboration:** "We'll brief you the day before the shoot" or "5+ stakeholders need to approve"
- **Scope:** "Just send one person with a camera" (implies no crew, no technical depth)
- **Brand:** "We hire based on lowest bid" or "We own your footage forever" (IP risk)
- **General:** "We want the world's best but pay for student pricing" (expectation mismatch)
