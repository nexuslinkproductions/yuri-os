## Quick Reference — Signal Types

| Type | Example | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| **INBOUND** | Email from agency asking about rates | Usually high-signal; respond quickly |
| **PARTNER-REF** | Claudio recommends a potential client | Very high signal; prioritize introduction |
| **WEB-SEARCH** | Found production company on LinkedIn | Verify credibility; research before outreach |
| **TREND** | "DACH brands increasing on-set budgets" | Track for pattern; may affect strategy |
| **PARTNERSHIP** | Another videographer wants to collaborate | Evaluate fit; may strengthen ecosystem |
| **COMPETITOR** | New competitor in Vienna with strong portfolio | Monitor; competitive intel |
| **EVENT** | Production conference happening next month | Consider attending; networking opportunity |
