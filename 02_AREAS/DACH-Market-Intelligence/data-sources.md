## Data Quality Filters

**Keep (high signal):**
- Referrals from Claudio, Marc, MACL-ONE
- Companies with clear budgets (job postings, public campaigns)
- Premium brands (luxury, tech, B2B SaaS)
- Production companies with collaborative profiles
- Agencies with creative reputation

**Filter (low signal / noise):**
- Social media volume shops ("post 3x/week cheap content")
- Ultra-discount providers ("cheaper than anyone")
- Unclear business models (MLM-adjacent, dropshipping)
- Copy-paste generic pitches
- No clear decision-maker

## Automation Opportunities

**Can automate (with external tools):**
1. **Google Alerts** (free):
   - Set alerts for: "production company Austria", "videographer Switzerland", "Werbung Deutschland"
   - Receives email digest; forward to signals inbox

2. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** (paid, ~$80/month):
   - Search: production companies, content agencies, brand marketing managers in DACH
   - Set saved searches; get weekly activity feed
   - Can identify new decision-makers

3. **Feedly or RSSHub** (free/paid):
   - Subscribe to industry publications, company blogs, Vimeo/YouTube feeds
   - Daily digest of DACH production news

4. **Twitter/X Lists** (free):
   - Create list: "DACH Production & Advertising" (follow key influencers, agencies, publications)
   - Daily scan of list activity

**Can NOT automate (requires human judgment):**
- Scoring opportunities against framework
- Reaching out to partners for introductions
- Evaluating collaboration fit (requires nuance)
- Creating outreach strategy

- **Date:** 2026-04-17
- **Source:** [web search / LinkedIn / referral from X / event / other]
- **Type:** [company / opportunity / trend / partnership / person]
- **Summary:** [1-2 sentence description]
- **Location:** [city, region]
- **Industry:** [industry category]
- **Potential fit:** [high / medium / low]

### Details
- Who: [decision-maker if known]
- What: [project type, scope hint]
- When: [timeline if mentioned]
- Budget: [CHF estimate if any signal]
