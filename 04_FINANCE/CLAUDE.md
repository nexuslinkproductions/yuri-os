# 04_FINANCE — Context
Austrian EPU bookkeeping for Nexus Link Productions.

## Structure
- [YEAR]/Invoices-Out/ — issued invoices: INV-[YYYY]-[###]_[Client].pdf
- [YEAR]/Expenses/ — expense receipts: EXP-[YYYY-MM-DD]_[Vendor].pdf
- [YEAR]/Bank-Statements/ — monthly bank exports
- [YEAR]/Tax/ — Umsatzsteuer, Einkommensteuer, Finanzamt correspondence

## Rules
- All income billed under: Nexus Link Productions
- Bank: Erste Bank Austria
- Tax obligations: Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung quarterly
- Invoice naming: INV-[YYYY]-[###]_[Client].pdf
