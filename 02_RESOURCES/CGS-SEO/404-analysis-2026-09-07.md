## Honest assessment of what redirects are worth

I checked before recommending work, and the case is **weaker than it first looks**:

- **External backlinks: 30 in total for the whole site, every one pointing at the homepage.**
  None of the dead pages has a single external link. So 301-redirecting them recovers
  **no link authority** — there is none to recover.
- **Internal links: the site is clean.** Crawled all 117 sitemap URLs, found 93 distinct
  `/artikel/` links, and **92 of 93 work**. Only ONE genuinely broken internal link exists
  (below). The 306 are historical URLs in Google's memory, not live broken links on the site.
- 404 is not a penalty. Google drops these over time on its own.

**So: redirects are hygiene, not a growth lever. Do not expect a ranking jump from them.**

> **AMENDED 08/09/2026 — this verdict was measured on the wrong axis.** It is argued entirely from
> *backlinks*, and on backlinks it is correct. But a dead URL can also be sitting on **live query
> impressions**, which I never checked per-URL. `/artikel/guardian-angel4-owb/` turned out to be a
> 404 holding **4,343 impressions / 270 clicks** over 16 months and **263 impressions in the last 3
> months alone** — the single biggest non-brand earner on the site, dead. Redirecting it is a growth
> lever, not hygiene.
> **Rule going forward: before dismissing a redirect, filter GSC by page and read the impressions.
> "No backlinks" does not mean "no traffic".** Detail: `guardian-angel-2026-09-08.md`.

What they DO buy: anyone arriving from an old bookmark or a stale Google result reaches a
product instead of a dead end, and Google stops wasting crawl budget on 306 corpses.

## Recommendation, in priority order

### 1. Fix the one broken internal link  (5 minutes, do it)
`/begriffe/` → `/artikel/buegelsicherung/`.

### 2. Bulk 301 redirects  (1–2 hours, modest value)
Pattern-based, not one-by-one:

| Dead URL pattern | Redirect to |
|---|---|
| `/artikel/*sidecar*` | `/artikel/sidecar-holster/` |
| `/artikel/*pancake*` | `/artikel/pancake-holster/` |
| `/artikel/*ipsc*`, `*idpa*` | `/artikel/ipsc_idpa_holster/` |
| `/artikel/*iwb*` | `/artikel/iwb-holster/` |
| any remaining gun-model `/artikel/<brand>-<model>` | `/artikel/owb-holster/` |
| `/en/artikel/...` equivalents | the `/en/` version of the above |

Free plugin: **Redirection** (by John Godley) — supports regex, logs 404s going forward.
Do NOT blanket-redirect everything to the homepage; Google treats that as a soft-404.

### 3. The actual opportunity  (Phase 3 — this is the real prize)
Do not just redirect these. **Rebuild them as real pages.**

You have everything needed:
- the exact URL list of what Google already knows (this file)
- the 84 gun models + 24 lights in the WCPA configurator
- measured demand: those model queries already generate impressions
- and you are the only Swiss shop that mills the mould for each one

A page per popular gun model — `Kydex Holster für Glock 17`, `… SIG P320 X-Carry`,
`… Walther PDP` — pointing at the configurator with the right options preselected, would
target searches you currently show up for and cannot convert. Start with the top ~15 by
demand rather than all 84.
