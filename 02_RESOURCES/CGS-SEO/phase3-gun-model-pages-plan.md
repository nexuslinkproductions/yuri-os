## Page structure

**URL:** `/kydex-holster/<gun-slug>/` under a new hub page `/kydex-holster/`.
The hub links to all 15; each page links back and across. That builds a proper content silo
rather than 15 orphans.

**Per page:**
1. H1 `Kydex Holster für <Gun> – Schweizer Handarbeit`
2. Short intro naming the gun and what you make for it
3. **Gun-specific angle** (see below) — this is what stops them being clones
4. Table: the 5 holster types + prices, linking to each configurator product
5. The exact variants of that gun you cover
6. Light/laser combinations
7. Retention & carry options relevant to that gun
8. FAQ (2–3 questions that differ per gun)

### ⚠️ BINDING COPY RULES (owner corrections, 07/09/2026)

1. **NEVER imply civilian carry.** Carrying a firearm as a civilian is not legal in Switzerland.
   No "everyday carry", "verdecktes Tragen im Alltag", "auch ausserhalb des Wettkampfs tragen".
   Legitimate framings only: **Sport/Wettkampf (IPSC, IDPA), Training, Schiessstand, Transport,
   und Behörden / Sicherheitsdienste / Berufswaffenträger** (the Behördenrabatt audience).
2. **Retention = Retentionsschrauben.** Usually **2** screws · **IWB and Sidecar usually 3** ·
   **Pancake always 1**.
3. **Bügelsicherung / Level 2 is NOT retention** — it is an added **Sicherung** (safety), a separate
   mechanical part. Never conflate the two.
4. **No cross-fit claims.** Never state that one model's holster does or does not fit another model.
5. **Model and light lists are current scope and keep growing.** Always invite the customer to get
   in touch if their gun or light is missing — CGS will review adding it.

## Honest risks

1. **Doorway-page risk.** 15 near-identical templated pages are exactly what Google penalises.
   Mitigated by the per-gun angle, real variant lists and distinct FAQs — but the content has to
   stay genuinely different. If it degrades into fill-in-the-blank, it will hurt rather than help.
2. **Timeline.** Expect 4–12 weeks before ranking movement. This is not a switch.
3. **Shop is in vacation mode until early October.** Pages can and should go live now — indexing
   takes weeks anyway — but visitors will hit the "Bestellbereich pausiert" banner.
4. **Publishing.** I can create and fill these as **drafts**; publishing is a step René performs.
