# Personal AI Assistant — Deep Setup Questionnaire

> One copy per person. Marcel → **Yuri** (provider-backed). René → **Jeffrey** (fully local).
> Answer freely — bullet fragments are fine, German is fine (René: einfach auf Deutsch antworten). Skip nothing marked ★.
> Purpose: this is the "deep interview" that turns a generic assistant into YOUR assistant. Answers feed the persona file, the confirm-gate rules, the memory seed, and the second-brain ingest priorities.

Name: ______________ · Date: ______________ · Assistant: Yuri / Jeffrey

## S1 — Identity & day shape
1. ★ What roles/hats do you wear? (e.g. craftsman, owner, sales, parent, developer…)
2. Walk through a typical workday, hour by hour, roughly.
3. Where do most of your hours ACTUALLY go — and where do you WISH they went?
4. When are your peak-focus hours? When do you do admin/paperwork?

## S2 — Work inventory
5. ★ Your top 5 recurring workflows, step by step (e.g. customer inquiry → quote → order → build → ship → invoice).
6. ★ Which tasks do you hate or postpone the most?
7. What breaks your flow most often (calls, mail, searching for info, machine setup…)?
8. Which work can ONLY you do — and which could someone/something else do if it were good enough?

## S3 — Assistant jobs-to-be-done
9. ★ If it worked perfectly tomorrow: the FIRST 3 things you'd hand off.
10. What weekly wins would prove its value within 30 days?
11. ★ The hard NEVER list: things it must never do without asking (or never do at all).
12. Things it should just DO silently, no announcements.

## S4 — Communication
13. ★ Voice vs typing: what split do you expect? When voice, when text?
14. Language: German / English / Schweizerdeutsch? Should it SPEAK Hochdeutsch or attempt dialect?
15. ★ Tone: formal ↔ casual ↔ edgy. Write ONE example sentence of how it should greet you in the morning.
16. Default verbosity: one-liner answers, or full explanations?
17. How should it deliver bad news or errors? (straight, softened, with options…)

## S5 — Proactivity & interruptions
18. ★ May it speak up unprompted? What's the threshold (urgent only / helpful / chatty)?
19. Morning brief: yes/no? What belongs in it?
20. Reminders and nudges ("you wanted to call X") — welcome or annoying?
21. When you're deep in focused work: what, if anything, justifies an interruption?

## S6 — Latency & the conveyor (small-talk bridging)
22. ★ Acceptable wait: for a simple question? For a hard task (research, drafting a quote)?
23. ★ While it works on something longer: should it bridge with brief context-related small talk, give a progress note, or stay silent until done? What SHOULD it say while waiting?
24. If a task takes minutes: should it say "I'll get back to you" and notify later, or hold the conversation?

## S7 — Tools & surfaces
25. ★ Apps/programs you use daily (exact names — mail client, browser, CAD, shop software, spreadsheets…).
26. What should it be able to CONTROL vs only read? (draft emails? send them? calendar? files? browser? machines?)
27. Phone integration wanted (talk to it away from the PC)?
28. Special equipment/workflows it must understand (René: Kydex presses, vacuum former, order book / Marcel: dev toolchain, YURI OS, trading engine)?

## S8 — Data & privacy
29. ★ What may it read and index: email? documents? finances? customer data? chat history?
30. ★ What is absolutely OFF-LIMITS?
31. Cloud tolerance: strictly local only (Jeffrey default) — or which providers are acceptable (Yuri)?
32. May anyone else talk to it (spouse, employee)? What may it reveal to them?

## S9 — Memory & continuity
33. ★ What must it NEVER forget (people, preferences, business facts, measurements, recipes/settings)?
34. Give one real example of "it should have known this" from your life.
35. Should it remember conversations verbatim, or distilled facts only?
36. What should EXPIRE from its memory (old projects, one-off events)?

## S10 — Trust & verification
37. ★ What must ALWAYS be confirmed with you before acting (sending, ordering, quoting prices, deleting)?
38. Per task type: rather fast-and-occasionally-wrong, or slow-and-always-right? Name one task for each.
39. How should it handle uncertainty — guess and flag it, or stop and ask?

## S11 — Persona
40. ★ Name confirmed (Yuri / Jeffrey)? Voice: male/female, rough character (calm butler, dry-witted colleague, energetic apprentice…)?
41. Personality dials 0-10: humor __ · directness __ · warmth __ · formality __ · swearing tolerance __
42. Pet peeves: what ways of talking do AI assistants have that drive you nuts?

## S12 — Person-specific
**Marcel (Yuri):**
43. Provider constraints: which quotas/budgets may the assistant burn (Anthropic / z.ai / ollama-cloud), any daily cap?
44. Relationship to YURI OS: is the voice assistant the FRONT-END to all of YURI (fleet dispatch, trading, memory), or a separate lighter layer?
45. Activation: global hotkey / wakeword / hot-mic / menubar — what do you actually want to use daily?
**René (Jeffrey):**
43. Business flow: how does an order move from first contact to shipped product? Where's the friction?
44. Customer communication: volume per week, channels (email/phone/WhatsApp?), languages?
45. Key people it should know from day one (family, suppliers, best customers, the one guy who always pays late)?

## S13 — Success criteria
46. ★ Describe a perfect workday WITH the assistant, 6 months from now.
47. What single failure would make you stop using it?
