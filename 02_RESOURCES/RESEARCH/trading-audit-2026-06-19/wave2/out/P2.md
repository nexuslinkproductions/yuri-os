**4. CORRECT METHOD FOR EFFECTIVE-N (Ledoit-Wolf / Bailey-López de Prado)** [R — cited method]

**The method:** Given a T×N matrix of factor returns R, the sample covariance Σ̂ is noisy when T/N is small (T≈45 timestamps, N≈32 factors → T/N≈1.4, far below the T≥10N rule). The correct pipeline:

1. **Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage** (Ledoit & Wolf, 2004, "Honey, I Shrunk the Sample Covariance Matrix," *Journal of Portfolio Management* 30(4):110–119): shrink Σ̂ toward a structured target F (constant-correlation or single-factor): Σ_LW = δF + (1−δ)Σ̂ where δ is the analytically optimal shrinkage intensity minimizing Frobenius risk. This eliminates the spurious near-zero eigenvalues from finite-sample noise.
2. **Eigenvalue participation ratio** on Σ_LW: N_eff = (Σλ_i)² / Σλ_i². This is the effective number of independent bets.
3. **Cross-validation / CSCV** (Bailey & López de Prado, 2014, "The Probability of Backtest Overfitting," *Journal of Risk* 16(1):79–100): combinatorially symmetric cross-validation to detect if the Sharpe is an artifact of multiple-testing.
4. **Deflated Sharpe Ratio** (Bailey & López de Prado, 2014, "The Deflated Sharpe Ratio," *Journal of Portfolio Management* 40(5):79–80): SR̂₀ = (SR_max − E[max]) / σ(SR), penalizing for the number of trials N (which must be the EFFECTIVE-N, not the nominal factor count, because correlated trials are not independent trials).

**Target effective-N:** A credible multi-strategy engine needs **N_eff ≥ 5** (5 genuinely independent bets) to achieve a stable portfolio Sharpe of ~1.0 via diversification (Sharpe ∝ √N_eff for orthogonal bets with equal per-strategy Sharpe). Below N_eff=3, the portfolio is one bet wearing a costume. **YURI's N_eff≈1.1–1.3** means the "32-strategy portfolio" has the risk profile of a single directional bet.

**Why the current factor-circuit commutatorNorm is theater on this data:** `factor-circuit.mjs` builds rank-1 projectors from either metadata embeddings (category one-hot) or injected return vectors. With the 2 live signals (obs-momentum, obs-vol-regime), their return vectors are ±returns and ±volSeries from the SAME bars → commutatorNorm≈0 by construction → allCommute=true → the circuit returns the classical combination. The "non-commutative ordering advantage" is structurally impossible on collinear inputs. [V — factor-return-vectors.mjs:signalToReturnVector confirms both vectors derive from the same bars array]
