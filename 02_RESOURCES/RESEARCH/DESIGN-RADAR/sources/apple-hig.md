# Apple HIG

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/

## Signals

- Hierarchy first.
- Harmony across controls and surfaces.
- Consistency across contexts and devices.

## Useful patterns

- Strong first-screen clarity.
- Platform-aware controls.
- Clean spacing and understated materials.

## Apply to YURI

- Move the shell toward a calm, layered command interface.
- Use clearer section naming and fewer ornamental labels.
- Let the layout communicate the system before the chrome does.
