## 3. The signal problem — two layers

</RichText>

<Columns id="signal-layers">
<Column id="layer-a" label="Layer A — 32 price factors (1m)">

<RichText id="layer-a-body">

These **already exist** and drive every trade. The instinct "we need a 1-minute factor" is satisfied — 32 of them. The problem: they are all algebraic shadows of the **same 1m price series**, so they fire + agree together. **Effective independent bets (eff-N) ≈ 1.0–1.7** — "one bet wearing 32 costumes." 14/32 are negative after fees; none reach significance (|t| > 2.3).

</RichText>

</Column>
<Column id="layer-b" label="Layer B — 5 overlays (info-orthogonal)">

<RichText id="layer-b-body">

To raise eff-N toward ≥5: funding-carry, perp-basis, carry-vol, cross-asset lead/lag, social sentiment — different data, deliberately telemetry-only. **Validate-first (today, 735k rows): 0 / 5 predict returns.** They stay unwired. OFI (order-flow) still needs real-tape R². The orthogonality instinct is right; the candidates just don't validate yet.

</RichText>

</Column>

## 6. Path to real edge (owner-greenlit)

</RichText>

<Callout id="fix1" tone="decision">

**① ROOT CAUSE CONFIRMED — the stops are inside the 1-minute noise band.** The multi-horizon gate (ARMED: `OBSERVATORY_MULTI_HORIZON=1`) picks the shortest fee-clearing horizon (**5m**), whose expected move is tiny (~0.18%). The stop is set to `expMove × 0.5 ≈ 0.09%` — **smaller than typical 1-minute crypto noise (0.1–0.3%)**. The fast risk-exit checks at the 1-second tick, so any noise spike trips the stop before the trade can develop. Result in the latest window: **0 winners / 40 losers**, every trade noise-stopped at full size (~$13), avg adverse move only −0.045%. **The fix:** floor the stop at a noise-aware minimum — `stopPct = max(expMove × stopFrac, 2–3 × recentVolPct)` — so it always sits outside the 1-bar noise band. A stop inside the noise is objectively defective (not a judgment call); the exact multiple is the reviewer's Q1 below.

</Callout>

<Callout id="fix2" tone="info">

**② A factor that actually validates.** No current factor clears |t|>2.3 / DSR. Options: microstructure OFI on real L2 tape (needs R²), a calibrated signal→return-edge transform, or event/funding edges at longer horizons. Until one validates, arming the sizer is premature.

</Callout>

<Callout id="fix3" tone="info">

**③ Calibrate the sizer inputs.** Both `edgeMean` and `winProb` must come from walk-forward outcomes (AFL_LEDGER) before arming. Infrastructure exists (`factorCalibration`); it needs an edge to calibrate on.

</Callout>
