# YURI Trading Engine — Honest Overview

<Callout tone="warn">
**Paper-only. No real orders, ever.** Binance USDⓈ-M perp. Sizing DISARMED.
This plan is deliberately honest about what works and what does not — the goal is a real review, not a sales pitch.
</Callout>

## Context

A **math-first** (not vibe-first) quantitative paper-trading engine. It records factor signals, sizes them through
a principled Kelly/CVaR sizer, fills them in simulation (Almgren-Chriss + half-spread + venue fee), and feeds
closed trades back into a learn loop that scores every factor for real edge (deflated Sharpe, Brier) and graduates
only what survives. **The single most important fact: as of today, no factor carries validated edge, and the tape
honestly shows it.**

<Callout tone="bad">
The tape is **current and correctly wired** (live `ensemble-*` signals, today's timestamps, post-restart) — not legacy.
The losses are real and are the **mathematically-expected** result of two compounding problems, not a bug.
</Callout>

| Metric | Value | Read |
|---|---|---|

Winners are cut to **~$1.12** while losers run to **~$13.44**. Even a genuinely good signal loses money at 0.08:1 RR.
Layered on top: the signal carries **no validated edge** (every factor DSR≈0, hit-rate ≈ coin-flip) and is heavily
**short-biased** (35/40 short). **This is the engine correctly proving — at paper cost — that the current signal +
exit logic don't work. That is the system working, not broken.**

<Card title="Layer A — 32 price factors (1-minute)">
These **already exist** and drive every trade. The instinct "we need a 1-minute factor" is satisfied — 32 of them.

## 3. Sizing & gates — what controls risk

<Columns>

<Card title="The sizer (computeSize)">
Fractional-Kelly + CVaR/vol/drawdown throttles, **fail-closed** (no edge → 0). Just wired into crypto but
**DISARMED shadow** — paper still sizes by conviction until armed.

<Callout tone="bad">
**Phantom-edge finding (live shadow):** a strong ensemble vote (net≈0.8) produced a ~$6–7k "priced" size because the
sizer reads **signal intensity as a return-edge**. DISARMED = zero harm; it confirmed **both** sizer inputs
(edge + win-prob) must be calibrated from real outcomes before arming.
</Callout>
</Card>

<Card title="The gates">
**Energy gate (computeU): advisory.** Records ΔU, does **not** block trades (DISARMED). **Not** what holds back profits.
**Real skip-gates:** fee-hurdle (edge < round-trip fee), multi-horizon (move must clear 0.15% rt), hysteresis (cool-down).
These *reduce trade count* — correct on a no-edge signal (you don't want more trades on a signal that doesn't work).
**The bottleneck is edge, not gating.**
</Card>

</Columns>

## 5. What is genuinely missing — path to real edge

<Card title="① Fix the reward:risk first — highest leverage, lowest effort">
The 0.08:1 realized RR is a **structural defect independent of the signal**. Winners exit at ~$1, losers at ~$13.
Even a random signal breaks even at symmetric RR. This is a parameters/logic fix, not a research problem — the
single biggest unlock.
</Card>

<Card title="② A factor that actually validates">
No current factor clears |t|>2.3 / DSR. Options: (a) microstructure OFI on real L2 tape (needs R²), (b) a calibrated
signal→return-edge transform (size on real edge, not intensity), (c) event/funding edges at longer horizons. Until
one validates, arming the sizer is premature.
</Card>

<Card title="③ Calibrate the sizer inputs">
Both `edgeMean` and `winProb` must come from walk-forward outcomes (AFL_LEDGER) before arming. The infrastructure
exists (`factorCalibration`); it needs an edge to calibrate on.
</Card>

## 6. Open questions for the reviewer

<OpenQuestions>

- **Stop/target placement** — we set take = 2× stop from a volatility estimate. Sane for 1-minute crypto perp, or is
  the stop too tight (noise stop-outs) / target too tight (cutting winners)?
- **Short bias** — the ensemble calls short 88% of the time. Signal-construction bug, or reading a real regime? How
  would you diagnose systematic directional bias?
- **Position sizing at $10–15k/trade** — we cap 10% equity/position (paper). For real size, what Kelly fraction /
  max-gross / correlation haircut across BTC/ETH/SOL/SUI/WIF/AVAX?
- **Edge sources** — for a discretionary prop trader, which of {order-flow, funding-carry, lead/lag, sentiment, event}
  actually pays at intraday crypto? Where look first?
- **Time horizon** — is 1-minute even right for a human-reviewable crypto book, or does the edge live on 15m/1h where
  noise is smaller?

</OpenQuestions>
