### `music.equal-temperament-ratio` — 12-TET frequency map
- **Formula:** f_n = f_0 * 2^(n/12)   (n = semitones from reference; inverse: n = 12*log2(f_n/f_0))
- **In:** f_0 = reference frequency in Hz (e.g. A4=440); n = signed integer or real number of equal-tempered semitones
- **Out:** f_n = frequency in Hz (real); inverse yields semitone distance (real)
- **Deterministic:** Pure closed-form exponential of a rational exponent. No data, no embedding, no RNG — same inputs always give same float. CPU-only, O(1).

### `music.euclidean-rhythm` — Euclidean rhythm (Bjorklund onset distribution)

- **Out:** binary onset vector of length n (1=hit, 0=rest), the maximally-even distribution

### `music.polyrhythm-lcm` — Polyrhythm period & coincidence via LCM/GCD
- **Formula:** two cycles of length p and q (steps) realign every LCM(p,q) = p*q/GCD(p,q) steps. Number of downbeat coincidences per joint cycle = GCD(p,q). For a p:q polyrhythm over one bar, hits of voice 1 land at multiples of LCM/p, voice 2 at multiples of LCM/q.
- **In:** p,q = positive integer pulse counts of the two rhythmic layers
- **Out:** LCM = combined repeat period (integer steps); GCD = count of aligned coincidence points

### `wave.dft-coefficients` — Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) coefficients
- **Formula:** X[k] = Σ_{n=0..N-1} x[n] · e^(−2πi·kn/N),  k = 0..N−1.  Inverse: x[n] = (1/N) Σ_k X[k]·e^(+2πi·kn/N). FFT (Cooley–Tukey) computes the identical X[k] in O(N log N) vs the naive O(N²).
- **In:** x[0..N-1]: real or complex sample sequence (a time/position-ordered signal of any quantity); N: length
- **Out:** X[0..N-1]: complex spectrum. |X[k]| = amplitude at frequency bin k (cycles per full window = k/N per sample); arg(X[k]) = phase. Type: complex array.
- **Deterministic:** Pure finite weighted sum of fixed complex roots of unity; no randomness, no learned weights, no embeddings. Same input → bit-identical output (FFT == DFT to floating-point rounding). CPU-only.
- **Falsifiable test:** Input x=[1,2,3,4], N=4 → X=[10+0j, −2+2j, −2+0j, −2−2j]. Verified with numpy.fft.fft. Parseval check: Σ|x[n]|²·N = 120 == Σ|X[k]|² = 120. Any impl returning a different X[0]=Σx=10 or violating Parseval is wrong.
- **YURI use:** SPECTRAL SCORING channel for the Foundry candidate-generator: take any ordered numeric trace of a candidate program/artifact (per-step ΔU energy trace, token-length sequence, AST node-depth sequence, commit-size series, test-pass time series) and DFT it. The magnitude spectrum becomes a deterministic feature vector: dominant bin = the candidate's intrinsic 'rhythm/period', spectral flatness = how repetitive vs novel. Rank candidates by spectral signature (e.g. penalize a code-gen output whose ΔU trace has a strong low-frequency component = slow oscillating progress; reward a flat-then-decaying spectrum = monotone convergence). ### `wave.goertzel-single-bin-power` — Goertzel single-bin spectral power
- **Formula:** Recurrence: s[n] = x[n] + 2cos(2πk/N)·s[n−1] − s[n−2], s[−1]=s[−2]=0. After N steps: |X[k]|² = s[N−1]² + s[N−2]² − 2cos(2πk/N)·s[N−1]·s[N−2]. Computes one DFT bin's POWER in O(N) without the full transform.
- **In:** x[0..N-1]: sample sequence; k: target bin index; N: length
- **Out:** |X[k]|² : real non-negative spectral power at frequency bin k. Type: scalar float.
- **Deterministic:** Two-tap linear recurrence with a fixed cos coefficient; integer-driven, no RNG, no embedding. Magnitude form is phase-convention-independent (avoids the e^(−i) vs e^(+i) sign ambiguity). CPU-only.
- **Falsifiable test:** x=[1,2,3,4], N=4: Goertzel power per bin = [100, 8, 4, 8] for k=[0,1,2,3], EXACTLY matching numpy |fft|² = [100, 8, 4, 8] (verified). A wrong impl that returns k=0 power ≠ (Σx)² = 100 is falsified immediately.
