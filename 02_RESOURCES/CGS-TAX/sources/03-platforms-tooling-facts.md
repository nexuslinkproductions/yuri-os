## 1. Etsy and Swiss VAT (platform rules, VAT ID, fee invoicing, CSV)

Facts, Etsy primary:
- Etsy collects Swiss VAT on orders shipped to Swiss buyers, from inside or outside CH: "Etsy collects VAT on orders shipped to buyers in Switzerland. VAT applies whether the goods are dispatched from within or outside Switzerland, and regardless of their value." Etsy adds the tax to the buyer total and "remits it to the Swiss Tax Authority". (help.etsy.com/.../360000337247, 24/10/2026)
- Etsy's Swiss importer/VAT identity: "Etsy - UID VAT: CHE-373.086.513", address ETSY IRELAND UC, Badenerstr 172, c/o KPMG AG, CH-8008 Zürich (same page). Sellers shipping into CH must put this UID and the CHF order value and CHF VAT collected on the customs data when the VAT is over CHF 5. Not relevant for CGS's domestic parcels, relevant if CGS ever ships Etsy orders into CH from abroad.
- Etsy does not use the words "marketplace facilitator" or "deemed supplier" on the pages I could read for Switzerland. The article is headed "Selling to buyers in Switzerland". The deemed-supplier concept is the ESTV's: Art. 20a MWSTG (see ESTV text below).
- VAT ID requirement: "If you're located in the EU, Norway, Switzerland, or the UK and your gross sales meet or exceed the local threshold in the calendar year, Etsy requires that you add your taxpayer name and VAT ID", failing which selling privileges are suspended after the final reminder. The Swiss "local threshold" figure is NOT stated on the page. (help.etsy.com/.../360058652054) Add path: Shop Manager > Finances > VAT ID (360000337247).
- Etsy fee invoicing for Swiss shops: the seller-fee article has a group "VAT for shops based in Indonesia, Ireland, Mexico, Japan, Serbia, South Africa, Switzerland, and Vietnam": "Etsy is required to charge VAT on applicable fees regardless of whether you've submitted a VAT ID to us." Payment account shows the charges; "a monthly invoice on the first of the month" lists VAT-eligible fees and the VAT charged, plus a credit note for later refunds (help.etsy.com/.../360040584433). The Custom Fees page separately lists Switzerland among countries with downloadable VAT invoices, available on the 1st of the month for the prior month, in the Payment account (monthly invoices are PDF, in English, amounts in USD per the fee article).
- So for a Swiss seller the "reverse charge" route that applies to EU sellers with a VAT ID does not apply: Etsy charges VAT with or without VAT ID. The page does NOT say which VAT (Swiss 8.1% or other) or which Etsy entity issues the fee invoice. The Irish-rate remark on the page ("current VAT rate for Irish sellers") belongs to the Ireland group, not to Switzerland.
- Local evidence (CGS own order, cockpit memory): Etsy fee line items for a CHF 50.00 order were 7.50 (offsite ad, 15% of 50) + 3.25 (transaction, 6.5% of 50) + 2.66 (processing, 4% of 54.05 buyer total incl. VAT, + 0.50) + 0.21 + 0.22 + 0.05 = 13.89. The last three sum to 0.48, which equals 8.1% of 5.91 (transaction + processing) to the rappen. `[inference]` These look like VAT-on-fees lines at 8.1%, not on the 7.50 offsite-ads fee. Not confirmed by Etsy; confirm against the actual monthly VAT invoice.
- Where VAT collected by Etsy shows in Etsy exports: Etsy's Payment-account page labels it "Sales tax/VAT paid by buyer (Remitted)" ("any sales tax paid by the buyer that Etsy collects and remits to tax authorities") and says buyer-paid taxes "are included in the total amount paid by the buyer and then subtracted back out, they are not deducted from your listing price" (115014483627, 115015747228). Seller-fee VAT appears as separate fee lines "Vat on processing fees" and "Vat on seller services" (115014483627). The monthly-statement CSV (Shop Manager > Finances > Monthly statements > Generate CSV) has Fee and Tax amounts and an Info column (360016389113). A column-by-column official spec for the CSV: NOT FOUND. The older "Orders" CSV has a "Sales Tax" column per third-party descriptions `[secondary]` (blog.coupler.io/etsy-to-csv, craftybase.com).

ESTV facts that decide how Etsy is treated (primary, MWST-Branchen-Info 27, print dated 29/05/2026):
- Operator is deemed supplier if it brings seller and buyer together (Art. 20a MWSTG). Two supplies: seller to platform, platform to buyer.
- Domestic case: "Die erste Lieferung ... ist nach Artikel 23 Absatz 2 Ziffer 13 MWSTG von der Steuer befreit, sofern ... [die Plattformbetreiberin] im Register der steuerpflichtigen Personen eingetragen ist." Proof for the seller: a list of platform deliveries "unter Hinweis auf Artikel 20a MWSTG" from the platform is "ausreichender Nachweis". Seller may opt to tax the supply with the platform's consent (Art. 43a MWSTV).
- ESTV worked example: buyer pays 108.10, platform keeps a 20.00 commission without taxing it, pays seller 80.00: "Der Verkäufer erzielt und deklariert ... einen steuerbefreiten Umsatz von 80 Franken (Art. 23 Abs. 2 Ziff. 13 MWSTG)". If the platform invoices its commission with VAT instead, the seller's supply to the platform equals the buyer-paid amount excl. VAT (MWST-Info 27, fictitious-input-tax section, Beispiel 2, which defines "Kaufpreis" as the buyer-paid amount excl. VAT in that case). Etsy does charge VAT on its fees to Swiss shops, so the second variant is the likely fit `[inference]`; this decides CGS's Etsy turnover base and must be confirmed with the Treuhänder.
- Exports: if goods go straight abroad, the platform's own supply is exempt, but "Die ESTV behält sich vor, bei den Verkäufern zu überprüfen, ob diese ... über einen entsprechenden Nachweis verfügen. Kann ein solcher Nachweis nicht erbracht werden, wird die Steuer beim Verkäufer erhoben." So Etsy orders to foreign buyers still need export proof on CGS's side (see section 5).
- Invoicing: if the seller issues an invoice with VAT shown, it must name the platform and add a note such as "MWST 8,1 % mit der ESTV abgerechnet nach Artikel 20a MWSTG durch [Name und MWST-Nummer der Plattformbetreiberin]"; without it the seller owes the VAT shown (Art. 27 Abs. 2 MWSTG). CGS issues no invoices on Etsy orders (cockpit memory), so this only matters if it ever does.
- ESTV platform list (dated 23/10/2026, 20 pages) checked with pdftotext + grep for "Etsy", "Ireland", and Etsy's UID CHE-373.086.513: NO match. eBay Marketplaces GmbH (platform since 01/01/2025), Amazon EU Sarl and Zalando SE are listed. ESTV says operators must agree to publication to appear on the list (estv.admin.ch/en/vat-registration-for-platform-operators), so absence is not proof that Etsy is unregistered, but it also means the ESTV list cannot confirm Etsy as "im Register eingetragen", which is the exemption condition above.

CGS action: (1) Before 01/01/2027 confirm with the Treuhänder whether Etsy Ireland UC is in the MWST register (ask Etsy support for its MWST registration proof, or check the ESTV UID/MWST register search for CHE-373.086.513); until confirmed, treat the Etsy "exempt supply to platform" treatment as unverified. (2) Download Etsy's monthly VAT invoices (Payment account, first of each month) and check they show Etsy's UID with MWST suffix and 8.1%; only then book the VAT as Vorsteuer (konto 1170). (3) Keep the monthly statement CSV plus the VAT invoice as the Beleg per payout. (4) Do not enter a VAT ID in Etsy unless Etsy asks; it makes no difference to fee VAT for Swiss shops.

## 2. Etsy fee structure 2026 for a Swiss seller

Etsy primary (115014483627, 115015628847, 24/10/2026):
- Listing: "$0.20 per listing", also per auto-renew and per extra quantity sold; listing lasts 4 months. Fixed fees are published in USD and converted to the payment-account currency at market rate when posted.
- Transaction fee: "6.5% of the total order amount" in the listing currency, applies to shipping and gift wrap too.
- Payment processing, Switzerland: "4% + 0.50 CHF" (country table, 115015628847), taken from the sale price including shipping and any sales tax charged. "Etsy Payments processing fees are subject to VAT."
- Offsite Ads: under $10,000 sales in the past 365 days a 15% fee on the order total; at or above $10,000, 12%; never more than $100 per order. Optional for most sellers, mandatory above the threshold per Etsy's seller page `[secondary on the mandatory part]`.
- Currency conversion: "A 2.5% fee is assessed when currency conversion is required" (listing currency different from payment-account currency).
- No regulatory operating fee for Switzerland (listed only for UK, France, Italy, India, Spain, Türkiye, Vietnam, Canada).
- VAT lines: "Vat on processing fees" and "Vat on seller services" ("VAT may vary").
- Third-party summary of the Swiss total with 8.1% tax: growtsy.com/etsy-fees/switzerland `[secondary]`; it claims 8.1% on all fees even with a tax number. Rate per fee type not published by Etsy.

Bezugsteuer relevance `[inference, to confirm with sibling lane/Treuhänder]`: all Etsy fees are services bought from Etsy Ireland UC. If Etsy bills them with Swiss VAT under its Swiss UID, they are normal domestic purchases (Vorsteuer, Ziffer 400) and no Bezugsteuer arises. If any fee line is billed without Swiss VAT (for example offsite ads, which I could not confirm either way) it is a foreign service purchase and falls under Bezugsteuer (Ziffer 383, declared at 8.1%, with matching Vorsteuer in Ziffer 400 on the effective method). Etsy's help text does not tell which. NOT FOUND: a sample Etsy fee invoice for a Swiss shop.

CGS action: pull one real Etsy monthly VAT invoice and check line by line which fees carry VAT; any fee with zero VAT is a Bezugsteuer candidate. Book Etsy's gross payout/fees per payout with the statement as Beleg.

## 3. WooCommerce Swiss VAT setup (custom-gear.ch)

WooCommerce core, woocommerce.com/document/setting-up-taxes-in-woocommerce/ (24/10/2026):
- "Prices entered with tax": options "Yes, I will enter prices inclusive of tax" / "No, I will enter prices exclusive of tax"; it "determines how you enter product prices". Changing it does not rewrite stored prices (troubleshooting-core-taxes page).
- "Calculate tax based on": customer shipping address (default), customer billing address, or shop base address. With shop base, "taxes are always calculated based on your store's location, regardless of the customer's address", so for exports use shipping address.
- Shipping tax class: default "Shipping tax class based on cart items"; alternatives Standard / Reduced / Zero rate.
- Display prices in shop and in cart/checkout: "Including tax" or "Excluding tax". Price display suffix supports `{price_including_tax}` and `{price_excluding_tax}` placeholders (use for "inkl. MwSt." text).
- Rate table fields: country code, rate %, priority ("only the first matching rate at each priority"), shipping checkbox ("enabled by default").
- Non-CH customers: the core doc says only that with rates defined for a few countries, others are not taxed ("taxes are not charged"). With prices entered inclusive, the default behaviour for a customer whose location has a different rate is to remove the shop's base tax and apply the customer's rate: the woocommerce.com "Keep tax-inclusive product prices" page describes the filter as stopping "removing the store's base tax and applying the customer's location tax"; `add_filter('woocommerce_adjust_non_base_location_prices','__return_false')` keeps the gross price constant instead. For export zero-rating, the default (remove 8.1% from the price) is what you want; do NOT apply the filter unless you intend to keep the gross price and book the 8.1% as extra export margin. The filter is flagged experimental `[secondary: wp-kama.com]`.
- Zero rate for exports: not documented by WooCommerce itself. vendidero (Germanized maker) advises for third countries such as Switzerland, calculate by customer address and "für Drittländer keinen Steuersatz hinterlegen" or explicitly set 0%; delivery to a third country is tax-free "insofern ihr die Lieferung nachweisen könnt" (vendidero.de/mehrwertsteuer-in-woocommerce-alles-was-du-wissen-musst). Wildcard rate `*` applies to every country without its own rate; vendidero recommends one per tax class. Shipping must have a 0% row too or the zero rate must cover the shipping checkbox.

Germanized and Switzerland:
- Germanized is built for the German market. Its small-business toggle is "Kleinunternehmerregelung (§19 UStG)" under WooCommerce > Settings > Germanized. When on, vendidero says tax calculation is switched off (the "Mehrwertsteuer" tab disappears) and a §19 UStG note is added `[vendidero blog + wordpress.org support thread, secondary for the UI wording]`. A Swiss equivalent does not exist in the plugin; NOT FOUND any Germanized documentation naming Switzerland.
- Consequence for CGS `[inference]`: the shop currently runs with 8.1% rows either entered or not (not verified); after 01/01/2027 the §19 toggle must stay OFF, and the "Kein Mehrwertsteuerausweis" notice must not appear. Germanized has a "Drittland" invoice shortcode `[third_party_country]` (Germanized Pro) for non-EU destinations.
- Local note: the site lists Germanized, WooPayments, TWINT, WCPA among ~41 plugins (cockpit memory). Which plugin generates the customer invoice/PDF is not in my local notes: NOT FOUND.

Invoice fields for Art. 26 MWSTG (ESTV MWST-Branchen-Info 27 Ziff. 2.7, 29/05/2026, lists the standard elements):
- a. supplier name and place, note that it is entered in the MWST register, and the number; b. recipient name and place; c. date or period of service if different from invoice date; d. kind and extent of service; e. consideration; f. tax rate and tax amount; if the price includes tax the rate suffices.
- "PDF Invoices & Packing Slips" (WP Overnight): shop VAT number is a plugin setting under WooCommerce > PDF Invoices > General, printed with placeholder `{{wpo_wcpdf_shop_vat_number}}`; per-item "Tax rate" column and "VAT" blocks in the totals (tax base and tax percentage) exist only in the paid Premium Templates; the free version follows WooCommerce tax settings, showing "(includes $24.10 VAT)" with tax-inclusive prices and itemised rates only if "Display tax totals" is enabled (docs.wpovernight.com/.../displaying-taxes/). Version 5.13.0 (01/06/2026) of the free plugin added support for the WC EU VAT Number plugin `[secondary: wordpress.org changelog via search]`. The UID format with MWST suffix: the field is free text, so enter "CHE-442.561.192 MWST" yourself.

Acowebs WCPA add-ons and tax:
- Official changelog (acowebs.com/woo-custom-product-addons/) lists: 3.3.0 "the price will be showing based on the woocommerce tax settings"; 5.0.12 "Set tax class for addons"; 5.8.2 "Block Cart and Checkout rows show the add-on price in every 'Tax for addon' mode". So WCPA has a per-add-on tax class and a "Tax for addon" setting with several modes, but the modes and the calculation are NOT documented in anything I could fetch (wordpress.org free-plugin page has no tax entries). Do not assume add-ons inherit the product tax class; test it.

MwSt." via price suffix; rows for each export country at 0% (or none) and keep the default base-tax removal; Germanized §19 toggle OFF; set shop VAT number in the invoice plugin; test an order with a WCPA add-on to CH, DE and US addresses and check tax per line and shipping.

## 5. Swiss Post, UPS and DHL exports: documents and Ausfuhrnachweis

Swiss Post primary (post.ch, 24/10/2026):
- Customs declaration for small goods: CN22 for goods value up to CHF 1,000 (parcels/Kleinwaren up to 2 kg); CN23 above CHF 1,000 or if the sender does not want to show the value; data entry must be electronic. Post offers free online services ("Begleitpapiere Kleinwaren International"); staff data entry costs CHF 5.00 per item. Commercial invoice is required for goods with commercial value. For PostPac International and URGENT the customs declaration is part of the waybill (Frachtbrief). (FAQ, English accompanying-documents page). The page shows a CN23 threshold inconsistency (CHF 400 vs CHF 1,000) between headings and body.
- Export declaration (Ausfuhrdeklaration) is needed above CHF 1,000 goods value or for goods needing an export permit; it is part of the waybill for PostPac International/URGENT.
- Who files: for most customers "Post CH Ltd files the clearance on its own ZAZ account", decisions come from the Swiss Post platform; if the customer files its own e-dec Export, the eVV goes straight from the BAZG to the customer (Swiss Post eVV page). Products with eVV: Kleinwaren International, PostPac International, Swiss Post GLS parcels, EMS parcels.
- eVV (elektronische Veranlagungsverfügung) is "ein Verzollungsnachweis, den das Bundesamt für Zoll und Grenzsicherheit ausstellt"; normally ready within 72 hours (FAQ) / three working days (FAQ elsewhere) / four to five days (eVV page); download at post.ch/evd, free for 3 months after clearance, retrievable later for a fee; retroactive eVV only if no declaration was filed, goods value at least CHF 1,000, processing date at most 60 days back. VAT-registered taxpayers must keep the eVV electronically for 11 years (10 plus current year).
- Waiver of eVV (Swiss Post FAQ, exact German): "Bei der Ausfuhr von Gegenständen, die keiner Bewilligungspflicht unterliegen und einen Warenwert bis CHF 1'000.00 haben, ist keine eVV nötig. Unternehmen, die beim ... BAZG als MWST-pflichtige Personen eingetragen sind, können in diesen Fällen die Ausfuhr gegenüber der ESTV wie folgt nachweisen: mit dem Aufgabenverzeichnis / der Aufgabenquittung der Post sowie mit den relevanten Geschäftsunterlagen (z.B. Bestellung, Rechnungskopie, Liefervertrag, Zahlungsbeleg)."
- ESTV primary on proof: MWST-Info 04 (27/05/2026) says exports are exempt "sofern der Nachweis der Ausfuhr erbracht werden kann" and "Im Mehrwertsteuerrecht gilt der Grundsatz der freien Beweiswürdigung (Art. 81 Abs. 3 MWSTG), d. h. die Ausfuhrbefreiung kann auch anderweitig geltend gemacht werden. Es wird jedoch empfohlen, die Zolldokumente auch für die Belange der MWST aufzubewahren." MWST-Info 06 and MWST-Branchen-Info 09 use the formula "vorzugsweise mit einer Veranlagungsverfügung des BAZG". BAZG: accepted proof is the "elektronische Ausfuhrveranlagungsverfügung (e-dec Export) mit digitaler Signatur"; "Wenn Ausfuhren nicht belegt werden können, sind die Lieferungen im Inland zu versteuern". I did not find an ESTV page that itself repeats the Post's CHF 1,000 postal-receipt rule; it comes from Swiss Post (quoting the ESTV practice) and an older practice note `[secondary: vatsupport.ch Vision 08/2014, finesolutions.ch calls it a 2008 ESTV practice, criticised as outdated]`. Treat it as accepted practice via free evaluation of evidence, not as a statutory safe harbour.
- Exact wording "Ausfuhrnachweis" is used by the BAZG (page title "Nachweis der Ausfuhr"). For CN22/CN23: they are customs declarations for the destination country, not Swiss export proofs.
- BAZG eVV Export retrieval: via receipt service (web service/e-mail, UID required), via Chartera Output with a ZKV certificate, or without registration with the declaration number and access code that the forwarder (carrier) gives the exporter; retrieval is tied to the UID of the exporter on the declaration. Export declarations moved to the new system Passar (documents from e-dec and Passar retrieved separately during transition); a Passar PDF is only legally valid with embedded XML `[secondary for the 2026 date: finesolutions.ch, douana.ch]`.
- UPS and DHL Express: NOT FOUND any UPS or DHL page on providing the Swiss export eVV or on Ausfuhrnachweis for MWST. What I found: DHL files customs declarations electronically for Express shipments from the commercial-invoice data (dhl.com Swiss shipping guides `[carrier marketing pages]`); UPS CH needs a commercial invoice for all non-document shipments (ups.com). The BAZG mechanism above (carrier gives declaration number and access code) is the route. UPS CH service guide lists a fee for a "Zollabfertigungsnachweis" sent on customer request (valid from 22/12/2024; fee amount not cleanly readable).

CGS action: (1) For every foreign parcel keep: the Post receipt/Aufgabeverzeichnis, the customer order, invoice copy and payment proof (matches the Post rule for goods up to CHF 1,000, no export permit). (2) Above CHF 1,000 (or any goods needing a permit) use PostPac/URGENT with the export declaration and download the eVV at post.ch/evd within 3 months; store the XML for 11 years. (3) Be registered with the BAZG as MWST-pflichtig before relying on the waiver wording (it is addressed to companies "beim BAZG als MWST-pflichtige Personen eingetragen"). (4) Same proof discipline for Etsy orders shipped abroad (MWST-Info 27 reserves the right to check sellers' export proof). (5) For UPS/DHL exports ask the carrier for the declaration number and access code.

## 6. Imports via UPS/DHL from the USA (Holstersmith): documents, eVV, industrial tariffs

- Deduction condition (ESTV MWST-Info 09, 27/05/2026): the importer deducts Einfuhrsteuer as Vorsteuer only if "Er ist im Besitz der Veranlagungsverfügung des BAZG, lautend auf Namen und Adresse der steuerpflichtigen Person (Importeur)" and is actually the importer with power to dispose of the goods (supported by a booked supplier invoice). "Die vom BAZG ausgestellte Veranlagungsverfügung gilt als Beweismittel"; the entitlement arises at the end of the period in which the declaration was assessed. ESTV eVV page: for imports the eVV "dient ... als Nachweis für den Vorsteuerabzug in der MWST-Abrechnung" and must be archived electronically.
- Practical rule from BAZG sources: the import eVV must be fetched and kept electronically (obligation since 01/03/2018; print-out has no legal character) `[secondary: BAZG PDF title seen in search, consultive/finesolutions summaries]`. A normal e-dec import yields two decisions: eVV Zoll and eVV Mehrwertsteuer; the MWST one is the Vorsteuer proof `[secondary]`.
- How to obtain it (BAZG, "Bezug von eVV aus Passar"): three ways. (a) Customs software via API with ePortal role "Fracht"; (b) free web application "Chartera Output" in the ePortal with registration, role "Dokumentenbezug im Warenverkehr"; (c) without registration: "Verlangen Sie von Ihrem Zolldienstleister (z.B. Spediteur) die Nummer der Zollanmeldung" and the matching access code, then use the access function in Chartera Output (single queries only). During the transition, e-dec and Passar documents are retrieved separately. The ePortal menu name "Veranlagungsverfügungen abrufen" is NOT FOUND on the pages I read; the names are as listed above. Retention: 11 years (Swiss Post FAQ, same legal rule).
- Swiss Post imports use the online service eVD at post.ch/evd; UPS: the carrier is the customs agent that files the declaration. NOT FOUND: UPS page describing the "Zoll- und Steuerrechnung" layout. UPS CH fee guide (valid from 22/12/2024, tariffs excl. MwSt): entry preparation fee CHF 7.05 per import shipment when UPS does not pre-pay duties/taxes; disbursement ("Vorlageprovisions") commission 3.00% of import charges, min CHF 25.15 where UPS advances duties and taxes; "Zuschläge und Zusatzleistungen verstehen sich ohne Steuern. Es ist gegebenenfalls eine Mehrwertsteuer in Höhe des Normalsatzes zu berechnen". So UPS brokerage fees are domestic services with 8.1% VAT on a UPS invoice (Vorsteuer, Ziffer 400), whereas the advanced Einfuhrsteuer is Vorsteuer only via the BAZG eVV, not via the UPS bill `[inference from the two ESTV/UPS statements]`.
- Industrial tariffs: abolished 01/01/2024. BAZG: "Die Aufhebung der Industriezölle umfasst Waren der Kapitel 25-97" of the tariff, excluding some products in chapters 35 and 38 classified as agricultural; no origin proof needed for goods staying in Switzerland. SECO: "sonstige Abgaben und Steuern (z.B. Mehrwertsteuer, Mineralölsteuer) weiterhin fällig"; agricultural chapters 1-24 keep duties. Chapters 39 (plastics sheets, e.g. Kydex, HDPE), 73 (steel articles incl. screws) and 83 (metal fittings) lie inside 25-97, so duty is 0% `[inference: I did not classify the actual tariff numbers; check with the Tares customs tariff for each SKU]`. Only Einfuhrsteuer 8.1% (7.7% rate applies for imports before 2024) plus carrier fees remain.

CGS action: for each Holstersmith or other US import: make UPS give the declaration number and access code (or register in the ePortal role "Dokumentenbezug im Warenverkehr"), download the eVV (PDF with embedded XML), confirm importer name/address and UID match CGS, store it with the landed-cost sheet. Book Einfuhrsteuer from the eVV as Vorsteuer 1170 or 1171 (material vs investment), UPS brokerage VAT from the UPS invoice.

## 7. ESTV ePortal "MWST-Abrechnung pro": form Ziffern and XML upload

Access (estv.admin.ch/de/mwst-online-abrechnen, page dated 22/09/2026): online filing mandatory from 01/01/2025 ("Ab dem 1. Januar 2025 müssen alle MWST-pflichtigen Unternehmen die MWST online via ePortal abrechnen"); since 11/05/2026 only "MWST-Abrechnung pro" (the earlier "easy" is gone); login via myESTV with AGOV, select company, add service "MWST-Abrechnung pro". XML upload exists: "ausschliesslich im Format des E-Government Standards eCH-0217 Spezifikation E-MWST Version 2.0.0"; other versions not supported; check data after import. eCH-0217 V2.0.0 covers effective, Saldo and Pauschal methods; it does NOT carry the Einlageentsteuerung list, form 764 or other attachments (ech.ch/de/ech/ech-0217/2.0.0 summary). Predecessor 1.0.1 retired 27/02/2024. Software with export: Bexio, Abacus, Pinus, Dr. Tax, Finito `[secondary]`; cgs-books export capability: not checked here.

Ziffern (ESTV form 0550 layout + MWST-Info 15, 27/05/2026; effective method). Rates: Ziffern ending 3 (303/313/343/383) are services from 01/01/2024 at 8.1%/2.6%/3.8%; ending 2 (302/312/342/382) are services up to 31/12/2023 at 7.7%/2.5%/3.7%.
- 200: total agreed or received consideration incl. opted services, notification-procedure transfers and services abroad (worldwide turnover); also includes everything deducted in 220-280; Bezugsteuer services are not declared here but in 383; non-Entgelte go to Part III.
- 205: portion of 200 from exempt services (Art. 21) for which the option (Art. 22) is used.
- 220: "Von der Steuer befreite Leistungen (u. a. Exporte, Art. 23)", plus services to privileged institutions; deducted from 200, no MWST, full Vorsteuer. Exports and, `[inference]`, the exempt Art. 20a delivery to a platform (Art. 23 Abs. 2 Ziff. 13) belong here; MWST-Info 27 does not name the Ziffer.
- 221: services abroad (place of supply abroad); proof only on request.
- 225: transfers under the notification procedure (Art. 38), upload form 764.
- 230: domestic exempt services (Art. 21) without option; no Vorsteuer for the related expenses.
- 235: reductions of consideration (discounts, rebates, bad debts).
- 280: other deductions (for example land value, margin-taxation purchase prices).
- 289: total of 220-280 (automatic). 299: taxable total turnover = 200 minus 289.
- 303/313/343: taxable consideration at 8.1% / 2.6% / 3.8% (lodging) and tax; 302/312/342 same for earlier rates. MWST-Info 15: tax is computed on the 299 total split by rate; with gross figures use the multiplier 8.1/108.1 (=7.4931%).
- 383 (382 old): Bezugsteuer on foreign services, declared at the statutory rate; consideration excludes MWST.
- 399: total tax owed (form: "Total geschuldete Steuer").
- 400: Vorsteuer on material and service expenses (domestic, Bezug and Einfuhr tax). 405: Vorsteuer on investments and other operating expenses. 410: Einlageentsteuerung (Art. 32), detailed list must be uploaded in the portal; also used for corrections after switching from Saldo/Pauschal to effective. 415: Vorsteuerkorrekturen (mixed use Art. 30, own use Art. 31, switch effective to Saldo/Pauschal). 420: Vorsteuerkürzungen for Nicht-Entgelte such as subsidies (Art. 33 Abs. 2). 479: total deductions, sum of 400-420 (415 and 420 are subtracted: form shows signs "+" for 405/410, "-" for 415/420).
- 500: amount payable, within 60 days after period end. 510: credit in favour of the taxpayer; ESTV pays within 30 days, subject to review, after the IBAN is entered.
- Part III: 900 subsidies/tourism levies, 910 donations/dividends/damages (Art. 18 Abs. 2).
