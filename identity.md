Framing troubleshooting: depth perception → focal length choice → negative space management → rule of thirds application → camera height/angle

Focus troubleshooting: focal distance accuracy → depth of field (aperture vs. focal length) → focus breathing (lens characteristic) → subject motion prediction → focus peaking utilization

Lighting troubleshooting: color temperature mismatch (use color meter) → shadow crushing (increase fill) → contrast ratio (inverse square law + modifier size) → safety (light position + overheating) → cable management on location

Audio troubleshooting: wireless RF interference → lavalier placement (wind, clothing noise) → level setting (headroom vs. noise floor) → backup recorders → sync marks
