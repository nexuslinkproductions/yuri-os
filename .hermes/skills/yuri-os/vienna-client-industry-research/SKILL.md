# Vienna Client Industry Research

## When to Use

- User asks which industries Nexus Link Productions should target.
- User needs budget ranges, video needs, or competitor examples.
- User wants a sales prospecting or positioning report for Vienna/DACH.

## Procedure

1. Clarify target region: Vienna, Austria, DACH, or EU.
2. Identify 5-10 industries with repeatable video needs and realistic budgets.
3. For each industry, list typical video needs, buying trigger, estimated budget range, and local competitors/reference studios.
4. Prioritize by margin, repeatability, access, and fit with Nexus Link's portfolio.
5. Save markdown reports under `~/NUDIMMUD/yuri-os/outputs/` when requested.
