### Content Structure

**1. Hook (1–2 sentences)**
- Provocative question or surprising stat
- "Why does every videographer get gimbal drift wrong?"
- "The one lighting setup that works in ANY location"

**2. The Problem (2–3 paragraphs)**
- What challenge did you face?
- Why does this matter?
- What does the reader care about?

**3. The Solution (3–4 paragraphs)**
- How did you solve it?
- Step-by-step breakdown
- Specific gear/technique details

**4. The Insight (1–2 paragraphs)**
- What did you learn?
- What surprised you?
- What would you do next time?

**5. Call to Action (1 sentence)**
- "Learn more about [topic]"
- "Try this on your next shoot"
- "Comment your approach below"
