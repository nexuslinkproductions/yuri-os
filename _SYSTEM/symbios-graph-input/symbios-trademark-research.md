## Legal Framework

### Sabel v Puma Doctrine (CJEU C-251/95)
The Global Appreciation Test governs all EU trademark conflicts. Three interdependent pillars:
1. Sign similarity: visual, phonetic, conceptual — assessed holistically, from memory (imperfect recollection)
2. Goods/services similarity: Canon criteria — nature, purpose, distribution channels, complementarity
3. Distinctiveness of earlier mark: stronger marks get broader protection

Key analysis for SymbiOS: "Symbi" is the dominant conceptual element (symbiosis). The suffix "OS" is descriptive (Operating System) and weak. EUIPO examiners will find likelihood of confusion with SYMBIO marks under Sabel v Puma.

### Insolvency and Trademark Survival
Under Austrian Insolvency Code (IO) and EUTMR Article 19, trademark rights form part of the insolvent estate. Marks survive automatically — administered by the receiver. Zombie marks block new filings even when the insolvent entity cannot enforce. Marks only lapse through non-renewal, surrender, or cancellation action.

### Nizza Classification System
EU trademark system divides goods/services into 45 classes. A mark only protects within registered classes. Exceptions: famous marks (Apple, Coca-Cola) protected across all classes. Symbio/SYMBIOS is not a famous mark — protection is class-specific.

### Canon Doctrine
Supplements Sabel v Puma for goods/services similarity. Lesser similarity in services can be offset by high sign similarity. Relevant for SymbiOS: even if Symbio Finland's goods are not identical, the near-identical sign compensates.

## Regulatory Bodies

### EUIPO — European Union Intellectual Property Office
Primary EU trademark registry. Located in Alicante, Spain. Administers EU Trade Mark (EUTM) system. 3-month opposition window after publication. Filing via eSearch / e-filing portal.

### Österreichisches Patentamt — Austrian Patent Office
National Austrian trademark authority. Offers similarity research service (written report in under 24 hours, cheaper than a lawyer). Free initial consultation available for founders.

### WIPO — World Intellectual Property Organization
International trademark registration (Madrid Protocol). For international protection beyond EU.
