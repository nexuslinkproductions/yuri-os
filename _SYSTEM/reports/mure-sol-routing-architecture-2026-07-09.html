04 · escalation law

Cheap is a tool, not an authority

The escalation boundary is defined before model ranking. A cheaper candidate cannot “win” a task it is forbidden to decide.

BREADTH → FRONTIER

Disagreement or incomplete evidence

If cheap samples disagree, confidence falls, or required evidence is missing, promote the work to Sonnet 5, GLM 5.2, or MiniMax M3. No endless cheap retries.

FRONTIER → CRITICAL

Blast radius crosses the line

Security boundaries, migrations, irreversible state, protocol contracts, and core architecture move to Terra/Opus/Sol with independent review.

CONFLICT → EVIDENCE

Models do not vote truth into existence

One identical retry, then cross-family adjudication. Executable tests and local evidence override confident model consensus.
