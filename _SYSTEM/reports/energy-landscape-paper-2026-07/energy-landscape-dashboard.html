strict-descent gate · function built, not yet deployed · ship 2026-07-23

Accept the move only if it lowers U.

YURI scores every proposed transition against a scalar potential U. If a move raises the system's uncertainty or claim-to-evidence drift, the gate rejects it before it runs. This surface tracks what is built, what is still a worked example, and what the sprint must prove.

what is real today

Built, simulated, or still planned.

One row per surface on this page. The energy function and the telemetry layer are built and tested. The gate is not wired into live dispatch, and no real trace has ever been written — so everything downstream of that is a worked example or a plan, and it is labelled as such.

what U measures

Nine components decide whether a move lowers U.

U is a weighted sum of penalties minus evidence credits. Each weight scales one measurable quantity and pushes the score up or down. The values are operator policy — hand-tuned, not learned. Drag a weight to rescale its contribution in the worked examples below.
