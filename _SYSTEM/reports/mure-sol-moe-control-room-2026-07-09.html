04 · invariants

What the learner can never override

01 · SOL OWNS INTENT

The seat preserves conversation state, goal spine, owner gates, and final acceptance. Expert lanes return evidence, not authority.

02 · R2/R3 STAY FRONTIER

No score, outage, quota, or learned weight may route important semantic work into DeepSeek Flash or MiMo.

03 · EVIDENCE BEATS VOTES

Independent tests, schemas, and local proof decide conflicts. More agents do not manufacture truth by consensus.

04 · LEARN INSIDE THE MASK

The MLP ranks eligible role × model candidates. It does not learn away protected paths, security floors, or owner holds.
