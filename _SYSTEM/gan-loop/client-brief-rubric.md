# Client Brief Rubric — GAN Evaluator

**Domain:** Client proposals, creative briefs  
**Context:** Pitches to Claudio (c2moviez), Marc (planzerfilms), MACL ONE  
**Target Score:** 7.0/10 or higher  
**Max Iterations:** 3

---

## Binary Gates (Fail on ANY failure)

- [ ] **Deliverable Specified** — Exactly what client gets (video length, format, file type)
- [ ] **Timeline Clear** — Shoot date, delivery date, revision rounds explicit
- [ ] **Budget Mentioned** — Cost or rate structure stated (unless pre-approved)
- [ ] **Client Name** — Who are we making this for? Stated clearly.
- [ ] **Creative Direction** — What is the video *for*? (Message, use case, audience stated)

---

## Weighted Scoring Dimensions

### 1. Completeness (25%)
**What:** Does the brief answer all client questions?

- **9–10:** Client could greenlight today. Every detail covered.
- **6–7:** Most questions answered. One or two details need clarification.
- **1–4:** Missing major pieces. Client would need to ask follow-ups.

---

### 2. Clarity (25%)
**What:** Could Claudio/Marc read this once and understand perfectly?

- **9–10:** Zero jargon. Exact timings. Concrete examples.
- **6–7:** Generally clear. One or two ambiguous phrases.
- **1–4:** Jargon-heavy. Vague timelines. Confusing structure.

---

### 3. Tone Match (20%)
**What:** Does it sound like you, not a template?

**For Claudio (c2moviez):** Direct, solution-focused, assumes video production expertise  
**For Marc (planzerfilms):** Collaborative, open to feedback, detailed process explanation  
**For MACL ONE:** Polished, aspirational, brand-aligned

- **9–10:** Perfect voice match. Could only come from Marcel.
- **6–7:** Mostly right voice. One or two moments feel generic.
- **1–4:** Template tone. Doesn't match client relationship.

---

### 4. Confidence (15%)
**What:** Does this feel achievable? Are we setting ourselves up for success?

- **9–10:** Clear scope. Realistic timeline. Budget leaves buffer.
- **6–7:** Mostly realistic. One ambitious element noted.
- **1–4:** Scope creep risk. Timeline too tight. Budget too low.

---

### 5. Next Steps (15%)
**What:** Is it clear what happens next?

- **9–10:** "Send this back with revisions by [date], then we shoot on [date]"
- **6–7:** Next steps implied but could be more explicit.
- **1–4:** No clear path forward. Unclear when/how to proceed.
