Did you know that you can literally let AI run your life now? As complicated as that sounds, it's not that hard. The setup is literally just files and folders. So three pieces. Claude.md is the instruction manual, how the assistant behaves. Memory.md is its notepad, what it should remember between sessions, and a resources folder for the detailed stuff it only reads when it actually needs it, which keeps it cheap to run. Then you want to stack it. The root is the constitution. Rules that apply to everything. Under that, workstations. Areas of your life like email, finances, your newsletter. Each one has its own three files and the rules add on top of the root. Inside a workstation, you can go one more level, a specific project, for example, like refinancing a mortgage. And then it does the real work. Feed it a credit card statement, it builds a categorized spending tracker. After a meeting, it reads your calendar, finds a transcript, and drafts a follow-up in your conventions. They'll write your newsletter and your actual voice if you've given it to principles. Two tips so you don't overbuild it. Start with two or three workstations, not 20. And keep the root file short. It loads every single session. Google already shipped their own version of it called Spark at the I.O. But you really don't need to wait for it because it's just files, folders, and markdown files, and you just need to wrap it around something like Claude Cowork.
