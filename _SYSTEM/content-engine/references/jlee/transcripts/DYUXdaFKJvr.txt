If you can't be bothered setting up Claude with all these skills and home packs that everyone's talking about, then here's three settings that you can do right now to make Claude actually useful. Number one is preferences in your general settings. This literally tells Claude how you want to behave by default. So for example, I tell Claude to list all its assumptions whenever it does my answers so I can tell if it's correct or if it's actually hallucinating and making everything up. Number two is memory. Everybody knows Claude remembers things, but not a lot of people know that you can actually edit them so you can add them, delete them, or customize them based on your project and role. So basically you don't have to re-explain to Claude why you're doing this and what it needs to remember. Number three is styles. Click the plus icon under the message box and you can literally tell Claude the tone and language that you want it to use. So formal, brand, casual tone. Just click the style you want and Claude will talk the way you want it to. So yeah, preferences, memory, and style, you'll get more out of Claude than the people who use it raw, but you don't have to do all this unnecessary setup like all these skills and prompt packs.
