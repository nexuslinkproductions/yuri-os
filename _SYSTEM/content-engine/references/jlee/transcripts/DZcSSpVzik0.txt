Most owners don't realize that they're paying for three jobs every single month that AI can do for 20 bucks. One is the front desk, the person answering the same 10 customer questions and triaging your inbox. That's a few hundred a month minimum. On the other hand, AI can draft those replies for you and answer those same customer questions for cents. So you basically read and send and it does the typing. Two is your marketing content. Paying someone to turn out your posts and captions, you feed AI your brief and it gives you a month of first drafts in an afternoon. You're not paying for the writing anymore, you're just editing. Three is admin. Copying invoices into a spreadsheet, chasing the late payer, reconciling the books. The road part of that is the most replaceable of all. AI plus a connection to your tools does it and flags what actually needs you. But unlike what this may sound like, this isn't a call to fire your whole team. The judgment, the relationships, the tastes, this is something AI can't really touch or replace. And so if you reprice that, then you can keep that person to do more effective work and focus on things that actually matter.
