Anthropic shipped a new Claude last week and everyone is talking about the coding score. But here's what matters if you run a business, not a code base. Welcome back to keeping up with AI and what it means for your business. The new model is Opus 4.8, and Claude says this is the first model that stops just agreeing with you. Handed something wrong and it pushes back instead of just nodding along. More than 10 times drop in overconfidence. So why does that matter for your coding score? Because when AI is writing your emails, cleaning your spreadsheet, or summarizing a contract, being wrong isn't the real risk. Being confidently wrong is that's the one you don't catch. A model that tells you when it's unsure is one you can actually trust with real work. Let's be clear though, this is Anthropics claims. There's still limited testing results given how recent it is. The coding jump is independently verified, but the admits when it's wrong part that should probably take a few more weeks to be verified.
