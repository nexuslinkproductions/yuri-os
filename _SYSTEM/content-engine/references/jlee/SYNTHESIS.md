## 1. The script skeleton (appears in every winning reel)

Seven beats, 40–68 seconds:

1. **Pattern-interrupt hook** (first sentence, no greeting): a specific claim
   or anti-take. "Fable 5 just went pay per use, and honestly, I don't care."
   / "Everyone kept asking for the competitor dashboard, so I'm just giving
   the whole thing away."
2. **Grounding recap** (one line): "So quick recap if you missed it."
   New viewers caught up in <5s.
3. **The machine, named** (the substance): exact stack, exact counts, exact
   cost. "Four pieces. A scraper, a transcriber, Claude scores every one,
   the dashboard rebuilds. Five bucks a month." / "six checks, plain code,
   no AI in the maths."
4. **The demo proof**: he SHOWS it working (screen recording) and points at
   the output ("Here's the actual log. Fable was driving at half four in the
   morning. Three hours later it was Opus.")
5. **The philosophy line** (the quotable, near the end): "You don't rent the
   genius forever. You pay for it once and get it to write the SOP down." /
   "the money's in the boring machine that watches and tells you what
   changed."
6. **The honest catch** (credibility move, kills the sales smell): "The
   honest catch is this doesn't make a cheap model smarter. It just stops
   the dumb expensive mistakes."
7. **CTA + time-cost frame**: "comment X and I'll send it over. Three
   minutes a week."

## 2. Hook taxonomy (ranked by plays)

| Hook type | Example | Plays |
|---|---|---|
| Give-away of something people begged for | "giving the whole thing away. The code, the guide, everything" | 41k (5,185 comments) |
| System flex with named stack | "reverse-engineers every competitor while I sleep" | 88k |
| Anti-take on fresh news | "Fable 5 went pay per use, I don't care" | 23k |
| Curiosity + utility ("steal this repo") | "One repo worth stealing every week" | 79k |
| Direct pain ("everyone hits limits") | usage-limit fixes | 5.5k |

Pattern: the bigger the promised ASSET (code, guide, checklist), the bigger
the comments; the bigger the SYSTEM flex, the bigger the plays.

## 4. What transfers into Marcel's pipeline (voice-compatible)

1. **The 7-beat skeleton works for scripts, but beats 2 and 7 change.**
   Marcel runs ZERO CTAs (hard rule, no "comment X", no questions, no
   invitations, ever). His grounding line is the decode sentence
   (VOICE-PROFILE §6), and his close is a flat statement: understatement or
   extrapolation jab. Any link lives in the profile bio, never in the post.
2. **Named-stack honesty is already our brand** — jlee names Apify/Scribe/
   Claude; Marcel names real numbers and public tools (GitNexus, CodeGraph
   stats). Same trust mechanism, stronger receipts.
3. **The honest catch is Marcel's honesty gate in another register.** "Built
   to say no" and "the honest catch is..." are the same credibility move.
   Use it once per scripted post.
4. **The boring-machine line IS the control-layer pitch** Lilly wrote. His
   genre proof that this framing pulls reach.
5. **Series framing**: "One repo worth stealing every week" → our spinoff
   ladder is literally that. "One engine worth inspecting every week" is
   the series the spinoff repos want.
6. **News-decoded at 34–68s**: three-items-per-week roundup ("Three things
   shipped in AI this week that actually matter") is a repeatable LinkedIn/
   Threads format fed directly by our radar.

## 5. What we still refuse

Comment-keyword bait, dot-padding, hashtag blocks, "DM me". His growth
engine, against our voice rules. The substitute: assets so real people
share them without being asked (the 88k post had 129 comments and 88k
plays on zero bait).
