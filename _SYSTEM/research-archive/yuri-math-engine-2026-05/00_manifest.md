## Core Thesis

Math becomes an operating substrate for YURI:

- algorithms route and optimize
- logarithms measure uncertainty and information
- graphs represent tasks, memory, tools, formulas, and dependency structure
- formulas become versioned artifacts with proof obligations
- external math ecosystems expand capability through governed adapters
- YURI promotion gates decide what becomes trusted runtime truth
