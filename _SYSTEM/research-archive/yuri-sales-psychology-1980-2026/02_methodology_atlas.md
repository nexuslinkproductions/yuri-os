## Operating Frame

Sales systems are treated as structured hypotheses about buyer movement. Yuri should map a conversation to the smallest useful lens, state evidence tier, then recommend an ethical next move.

## Core Systems

| System | Evidence Tier | Best Fit | Core Mechanism | Yuri Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SPIN Selling | BOOK_METHOD | Complex B2B, consultative services | Discovery through situation, problem, implication, need-payoff | Build diagnosis before prescription |
| NEPQ / Jeremy Miner | PRACTITIONER_SYSTEM + BOOK_METHOD | B2C/high-ticket, coaching, phone sales, founder-led selling | Neutral questioning, emotional self-discovery, buyer-led persuasion | Reduce sales resistance, ask emotion-linked discovery questions |
| Natural Selling | PRACTITIONER_SYSTEM | Relationship selling, network/referral contexts | Non-adversarial dialogue and buyer self-persuasion | Compare with NEPQ lineage; preserve non-pressure stance |
| Raving Fans | BOOK_METHOD | Retention, customer success, referrals | Define vision, learn unstated wants, systematize delight | Convert satisfied clients into advocates |
| Challenger | BOOK_METHOD | Enterprise B2B, complex buying committees | Teach, tailor, take control; mobilizer detection | Use when buyer needs reframing, not more feature information |
| Sandler | BOOK_METHOD | B2B qualification | Up-front contracts, pain, budget, decision, mutual fit | Prevent unpaid consulting and vague next steps |
| MEDDIC/MEDDPICC | PRACTITIONER_SYSTEM | Enterprise SaaS and strategic accounts | Metrics, economic buyer, criteria, process, pain, champion | Structure qualification and forecast risk |
| BANT | PRACTITIONER_SYSTEM | Simple qualification | Budget, authority, need, timeline | Use only as a lightweight checklist; avoid premature disqualification |

## NEPQ Cluster

NEPQ is treated as important practitioner material, not peer-reviewed proof. Yuri should study it for:

- neutral/probing question sequence
- problem awareness before pitching
- emotional consequences of inaction
- buyer self-persuasion
- tone control and conversational detachment
- objection prevention through earlier discovery

Yuri must also compare NEPQ against SPIN, Natural Selling, Sandler, consultative selling, and Motivational Interviewing. Where lineage or plagiarism claims appear in forums, record as `ANECDOTAL` unless source-backed.

## Raving Fans Cluster

Raving Fans belongs in both sales and post-sale psychology. Yuri should use it to ask:

- What customer experience is intentionally being created?
- What has the customer not said but likely expects?
- What would convert satisfaction into advocacy?
- What repeatable delivery system protects delight?
- What referral or praise signal is appearing?
