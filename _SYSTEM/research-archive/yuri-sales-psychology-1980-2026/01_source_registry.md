## Source Tier Values

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PRIMARY_RESEARCH` | Peer-reviewed paper, systematic review, meta-analysis, or formal research source |
| `BOOK_METHOD` | Published book or named method source; useful but not automatically empirical proof |
| `PRACTITIONER_SYSTEM` | Training company, field playbook, or operator framework |
| `CLINICAL_THEORY` | Clinical/depth psychology theory used as interpretive hypothesis only |
| `FIELD_REPORT` | Practitioner report, benchmark, interview, or industry case material |
| `VENDOR_RESEARCH` | Commercial research or trend report requiring incentive-aware reading |
| `ANECDOTAL` | Individual reports, forums, testimonials, or unverified claims |
| `REFERENCE_ONLY` | Link retained for context; not eligible to drive behavior without review |
