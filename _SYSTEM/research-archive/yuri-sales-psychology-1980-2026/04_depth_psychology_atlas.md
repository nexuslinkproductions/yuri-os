## Boundary

Depth psychology helps Yuri generate hypotheses about motivation and role dynamics. It does not diagnose buyers, infer trauma, or justify manipulation.

## Jungian / Analytical Psychology

| Concept | Sales-Relevant Read | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Persona | The role the buyer performs: expert, skeptic, protector, visionary, budget gatekeeper | Do not reduce the person to the role |
| Shadow | Disowned fear, resentment, envy, status anxiety, or risk avoidance that may appear indirectly | Treat as possible hidden conflict, not fact |
| Projection | Buyer places past vendor failures or idealized hopes onto seller | Validate history; do not argue with the projection |
| Complex | Repeating emotionally charged pattern around money, authority, trust, competence | Notice activation; do not label it clinically |
| Archetype | Guide, trickster, ruler, caregiver, hero, creator, orphan, rebel roles in the conversation | Use as symbolic map, not empirical proof |
| Individuation | Buyer moving toward a more integrated future identity | Relevant for high-stakes identity purchases |
| Compensation | What the buyer says may be counterbalanced by what they avoid saying | Pair with omission analysis |

## Sales Translation

Yuri should look for:

- identity threat: buyer fears the purchase says something bad about them
- trust rupture: past vendors make present claims harder to believe
- autonomy threat: buyer feels pushed or cornered
- status anxiety: buyer protects rank, competence, or public image
- meaning desire: buyer wants the purchase to serve a larger story
- belonging desire: buyer wants social proof, affiliation, or community
- competence desire: buyer wants mastery, confidence, or operational control
- avoidance: buyer dodges budget, authority, timeline, or emotional stakes
- ambivalence: buyer voices both change talk and sustain talk
- positive fan-energy: buyer moves from satisfaction toward advocacy

## Required Language

Use:

- "possible layer"
- "hypothesis"
- "signal suggests"
- "do not treat as proof"

Avoid:

- "the buyer is traumatized"
- "diagnose"
- "pathology"
- "exploit"
- "unconscious weakness"
