`What is Yuri prompt engineering doctrine, and what rules replace identity-roleplay prompts? Answer briefly.`

Answer:

Yuri prompt engineering doctrine defines prompts as operating contracts rather than performance scripts to avoid identity simulation. The rules that replace identity-roleplay prompts include:

- Using responsibility, evidence, and output contracts.
- Replacing phrases like "Identity-roleplay setup" with audit responsibilities.
- Applying the operating contract instead of pretend/performative setups.
- Optimizing for measurable criteria rather than being a genius.
- Decomposing problems step by step and reporting concise rationales, checks, and evidence.
- Producing outputs that pass specific rubrics or evaluation criteria.
