## Tax Set-Aside

**Rule:** 30% of every project payment goes into a separate bucket immediately.

```
Gross payment received:    € X,XXX
Set aside (30%):           €  XXX
Available to spend:        € X,XXX
```

**When a payment lands:**
1. Log it in project income
2. Calculate 30%
3. Move the 30% to the tax bucket (separate bank account or mental accounting)
4. Spend the remaining 70% as normal

**Don't touch the tax bucket for anything except:**
- Quarterly tax payments
- Annual tax filing
- Accountant fees

## Quick Reference — Know in 5 min

1. **How much money do I have right now?** (total liquid)
2. **What's my monthly burn?** (fixed costs)
3. **How much have I earned this month?** (revenue)
4. **How much do I owe in taxes?** (30% set-aside check)
5. **What's my buffer?** (savings - 3 months burn)

## Review Cadence

| What | When | Duration |
|------|------|----------|
| Log new project income | When payment lands | 2 min |
| Monthly update | First night of month | 15 min |
| Quarterly tax set-aside check | First of April, July, Oct, Jan | 10 min |
| Annual review | January | 30 min |

## Notes

- Burn rate is your floor. Revenue below burn = problem.
- 3 months of burn in savings is minimum buffer.
- Tax set-aside is not optional — 30% is the minimum for Austrian freelancers. Adjust if your actual rate is different.
- Trading is a separate system. Don't commingle with production income.
- If you can't answer "how much will I earn this month" within 20%, create a pipeline view below.
