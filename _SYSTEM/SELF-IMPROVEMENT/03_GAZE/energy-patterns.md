# Energy Patterns — Nocturnal Rhythm Tracking

> Track the night. Find the patterns. Optimize the block.

## Daily Entry

One line per night block. Takes 10 seconds.

### Template

```
YYYY-MM-DD | 21:30-04:00 | E:4 O:5 | Strong focus, no caffeine. Slept 7h. |
YYYY-MM-DD | 22:00-02:30 | E:2 O:2 | Distracted. Client stress. Slept 5h. |
YYYY-MM-DD | 21:00-05:00 | E:5 O:5 | Flow state 4h+. Japanese flashbacks. Slept 7.5h. |
```

**Fields:**
- `YYYY-MM-DD` — date the night block started
- `21:30-04:00` — start time → end time
- `E:4` — Energy at start of block (1-5)
- `O:5` — Output quality rating (1-5)
- Free text — what worked or didn't

### Energy Scale (1-5)

| Rating | Meaning | Tell |
|--------|---------|------|
| 1 | Dragged through it | Could barely stay seated, brain fog |
| 2 | Low momentum | Needed 30+ min to get going |
| 3 | Baseline | Got into it, nothing special |
| 4 | Good flow | Focused 2+ hour blocks, good decisions |
| 5 | Peak | 4+ hours flow, creative breakthroughs |

### Output Quality (1-5)

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 1 | Wasted the night |
| 2 | Got something done, but low quality |
| 3 | Met baseline expectations |
| 4 | Good work, proud of it |
| 5 | Exceptional — would pay for this output |

## Energy Management Rules

### What Fuels

- Consistent start time (same hour every night)
- Pre-block ritual (5 min prep: water, desk reset, single intention)
- Protein-rich early meal
- Japanese study as warm-up (low-stakes, gets brain in gear)
- Binaural beats / focus music

### What Drains

- Notifications during the block (phone on DND, Slack closed)
- Client stress spilled into system-building time
- Caffeine after 02:00 (interferes with 05:00 sleep)
- Decision fatigue (too many small choices during the block)
- Skipping the pre-block ritual

### Sleep Quality Tracking (Rough Only)

| Quality | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| 7h+ | Ideal — log as "Slept 7h" |
| 6-7h | Acceptable — note if it affected energy |
| <6h | Log the reason — adjust next night |
| Interrupted | Log cause (stress, caffeine, noise) |

**Rule:** Don't track sleep metrics (REM, deep sleep, Oura ring data). Track only: hours slept + subjective quality. Anything more is noise.
