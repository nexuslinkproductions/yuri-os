## Key Principles

1. **Read before you write.** Read the relevant file first. Costs < 200 tokens. Context wins.
2. **Don't scan the tree.** `_Directory_Map.md` costs less than iterating the filesystem.
3. **Routing is a cycle.** `03_GAZE/` results inform `00_VESSEL/` updates. If evidence contradicts a framework, update the framework.
4. **Default to Coworker.** Unless the task is deterministic and low-risk, keep the human in loop.
