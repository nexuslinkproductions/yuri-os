## Core Philosophy

### The Night Block Is the Engine
21:00-05:00. Zero interruptions. Flow state thrives here. Protect it like a client booked you.

### Transition Rituals > Willpower
Each schedule block needs a deliberate transition. Rituals signal the nervous system: *mode changing now.*

### The "No Guilt" Principle
You wake at 11:00. Your peak is 21:00-04:00. Standard productivity advice (5 AM club, morning power hours) is written for diurnal people. Ignore it categorically.

### 3-Task Maximum
One hard task + two easy tasks per sprint. Capacity is finite, not negotiable.
