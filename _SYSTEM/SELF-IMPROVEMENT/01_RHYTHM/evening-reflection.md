# Evening Reflection — 5-Minute Close Ritual

> End every night block with this. It's the cheapest system upgrade you'll ever install.

---

## The Five Questions

Answer these at 04:00-04:30 before shutting down. Keep answers short — one line each.

### 1. What did I actually produce tonight?
Not what you *attempted*. Not what you *feel like you should have done*. What exists now that didn't exist before?

- *"Refactor pass: modules 1-5 completed, notes written for modules 6-7."*
- *"Leetcode: solved 2/3 mediums. Third one pseudo-coded, attempted, didn't pass."*
- *"Nothing on the main task. Organized the reference library — 400 items keyworded."*

### 2. What blocked me?
Honest answer. No self-judgment. Just data.

- *"Wasn't sure which direction the client wanted — should have clarified this morning."*
- *"Couldn't get past the DP problem's edge case. An hour wasted."*
- *"Energy was low all night. Just a bad day."*
- *"Phone notification at 23:00 broke flow for 30 min."*

### 3. What's the first thing tomorrow?
The first 5-minute action. Specific enough that you could do it with your eyes closed.

- *"Open the editor, go to the project, re-open module 6."*
- *"Open leetcode, find the solution discussion for the problem I got stuck on."*
- *"Check email for the client reply before starting anything."*

### 4. Emotional check-in (1-10)
How do I feel right now? Not about the work — about *me*.

- 1-3: Fried, drained, frustrated
- 4-6: Neutral, okay, slightly tired
- 7-8: Good, satisfied, in flow
- 9-10: Excited, energized, wouldn't mind continuing

### 5. One thing I'm grateful for tonight
Prevents the night from turning into a tally of what you didn't finish. Pick anything.

- *"That refactor in module 4 actually works. First time trying that approach."*
- *"I made real progress today, even if I was rushing."*
- *"I have control over my schedule. That's worth remembering."*
- *"Good tea."*

---

## Journal Template

Copy-paste this into your night notes or log:

```
## 2026-05-05 (Tue)

### 1. Output
[what exists now]

### 2. Blockers
[what got in the way]

### 3. Tomorrow's Hook
[first 5-minute action]

### 4. State
[energy × emotion, 1-10]

### 5. Grateful
[one thing]
```

---

## Why This Works

**Output tracking** kills the feeling of "I did nothing tonight" — you always did something, and seeing it written breaks the negativity loop.

**Blocker tracking** accumulates into patterns: *"Oh, I always hit this wall around 01:00 on Tuesdays. I should schedule fallback tasks for Tuesday nights."*

**Tomorrow's hook** eliminates the "what do I do first?" decision next session. That's 15 minutes of waffling saved every single night.

**Emotional check-in** prevents burnout by catching decline before it's critical. If you log 1-3 for three nights in a row, the system tells you: rest now.

**Gratitude** is cheap and effective. One line. Don't overthink it.
