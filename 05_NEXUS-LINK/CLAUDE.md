# 05_NEXUS-LINK — Context
Nexus Link Productions company hub. Internal brand, strategy, legal, and identity documents.

## Subfolders
- Brand/ — visual identity, brand standards, logo files, color palette
- Strategy/ — business strategy, positioning, pricing, service design
- Legal/ — contracts, templates, IP agreements, terms
- Services/ — service definitions, packages, deliverables, pricing sheets
- Identity/ — company narrative, mission, values, positioning
- Portfolio/ — selected work, case studies, showreel references

## Key Principle
Nexus Link Productions is a production company — not a social media agency.
Premium · Futuristic · Precise. Marcel is a Video Producer, not a videographer.
