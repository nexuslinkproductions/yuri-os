- **Purpose:** Autonomous discovery of high-value on-set capture opportunities in DACH region

  - Tier 1 opportunities: `analysis/biweekly-analysis-latest.md`
  - Market synthesis: `reports/monthly-synthesis-latest.md`
  - Framework: `framework.md` (success metrics)
  - Data sources: `data-sources.md`

### How It Works
1. **Weekly (Monday 08:00):** Autonomous agent gathers signals from:
   - LinkedIn (videographers, production companies, agencies)
   - Google News & industry alerts (DACH advertising)
   - Partner networks (Claudio, Marc, MACL-ONE)
   - Inbound opportunities (emails, referrals)

2. **Biweekly (Wednesday 07:00):** Autonomous agent scores all signals
   - Revenue fit (CHF amounts, frequency)
   - Collaboration quality (decision speed, tech literacy)
   - Project scope (cameras, locations, crew complexity)
   - Brand alignment (premium positioning, repeat potential)
   - Output: Tier 1/2 opportunities ready for pursuit

3. **Monthly (28th 06:00):** Autonomous agent synthesizes trends
   - Industry patterns, geographic hot spots
   - Partner network health
   - Framework calibration (did scoring predict outcomes?)
   - Strategic recommendations

4. **Quarterly:** Manual strategic review with Marcel

### Who Should Pursue Signals?
- **Tier 1 (Score 3.5–4.0):** Hand to Claudio (c2moviez client relationship) or cold outreach
- **Tier 2 (Score 2.8–3.4):** Explore; clarify scope; request intro
- **Tier 3–4 (Score <2.8):** Monitor or filter
