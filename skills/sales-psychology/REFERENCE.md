## Named Clusters

- SPIN: diagnosis before prescription.
- NEPQ / Jeremy Miner: neutral emotional questioning and buyer-led self-persuasion; practitioner tier unless independently supported.
- Natural Selling / Michael Oliver: non-adversarial conversational lineage.
- Raving Fans: unstated wants, expectation design, delight, referrals, advocacy.
- Challenger: reframing and mobilizer logic in complex B2B.
- Sandler: mutual qualification and up-front contracts.
- MEDDIC/MEDDPICC: enterprise qualification and forecast discipline.
- JOLT: late-stage indecision and fear-of-failure handling.
- Motivational Interviewing: ambivalence and change talk.
- Jungian/depth psychology: persona, shadow, projection, archetype, meaning, symbolic role.

## Standard Next Move For Mixed Positive/Omission Signals

When the buyer praises the offer and asks implementation questions but avoids budget/authority/timeline:

```text
Confirm the fit, then clarify decision criteria before closing.
```

Useful questions:

```text
It sounds like the fit is real. What budget range would make this practical on your side?
Who else needs to be involved before this becomes a yes or no?
If this starts with one campaign, what date would make the first delivery useful?
```

## Safety

If a requested sales move uses false scarcity, pressure, shame, deception, or vulnerability exploitation, refuse that move and offer a clean alternative.
