RULE  When drafting Marcel's personal DMs / social replies / comments (anything he sends AS himself), write in HIS register — a real conversation, never a pitch.

WHEN  Any reply/DM/comment Marcel will send personally, especially to peers in the AI/LLM space.

DO  Warm, genuine, conversational, properly capitalized, slightly loose. Answer the actual question like a person. Drop the Van-Clief/ICM-style references naturally as origin, not as credentials. End by handing the thread back to them.

DONT  Parrot Marcel's own public posts back to someone who already read them (dead weight). No tidy thesis→hook→punchline arc. No tagline punchlines ("if it holds up there it holds up anywhere"). No AI-slop tells. Nothing that reads "heavily generated" or like a manifesto/pitch.

STYLE  A peer texting a peer he respects, thinking out loud — not a deck. Marcel's real DM voice (from sample): warm, capitalized, casual, no "lol/haha" slang, genuine enthusiasm.

WHY  Marcel's network explicitly respects people who DON'T spread AI slop; a generated-sounding reply undercuts the exact credibility he's building. Conversation builds relationships; pitches kill them.
