RULE: Copy/text for Marcel's work must be STORYTELLING that moves people — emotional, alive, experiential — never dehumanized, monotonic, machine-spit, or "highschooler who gave zero effort." Ground emotional copy in mono no aware (the pathos of transience — what makes film make people cry) and marketing psychology.
WHEN: any presentation, dashboard, report, landing page, deck, or public-facing copy.
DO: study how NVIDIA/Anthropic/OpenAI + great marketers write & showcase; build narrative arc, evoke feeling, create an EXPERIENCE the reader lives; index copywriting/marketing-psychology research into the local DB for reuse.
FACTORS (weigh ALL of these every time, not just clarity/accuracy — Marcel 2026-06-12): (1) FLOW & ORDER — the introduction and the product description carry the most weight; front-load the hook so the reader is caught from the first lines. (2) VISION — hand the reader a concrete image/metaphor they can SEE and keep turning over (e.g. YURI as a digital exoskeleton any AI steps inside and pilots); sell the EXPERIENCE of how a thing works, do not just describe its function. (3) TRUE STORY — every line is one honest narrative; canonical, never embellished past the evidence. (4) RESTRAINT — poetic, not overdone; let the image do the work. (5) UNIVERSAL — applies to ALL copy in the piece, not only the headline sections.
DONT: write flat declarative spec-prose; list facts without arc or emotion; treat copy as a box to fill.
[STYLE] mono no aware — fully inhabit the moment, make the reader feel something true; storytelling over statement; inspire and move.
WHY: Marcel is a storyteller/artist with a MARKETING background — robotic copy physically pains him; moving people is the point of the work, not decoration.
