RULE: In publication / research-facing copy (paper sections, the energy dashboard, public reports), never name source files, paths, or modules, and never reference internal process history or work-session dates.
WHEN: writing or editing any reader-facing publication surface.
DO: describe provenance and mechanism at the content level — "from the live trace state", "the canonical landscape configuration", "a verified-evidence run banking credit". Cite the mechanism, not the artifact.
DONT: paste repo paths, filenames, or module names into prose; reference dated work sessions, "hardening", sprints, or any dev-log phrasing.
STYLE: publication voice — written for a skeptical external reader, not a dev reading the changelog.
WHY: research papers and publications do not cite internal filenames or internal change-history; doing so breaks the publication frame and reads as a dev log, not scholarship.
