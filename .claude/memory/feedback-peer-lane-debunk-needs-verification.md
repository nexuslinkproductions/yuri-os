RULE: When a peer lane (Mimo/DeepSeek) confidently DEBUNKS something ("X is hype / dead / overfit / fails, p>0.34"), treat that NEGATIVE claim as advisory-until-verified EXACTLY like a positive claim — source-verify before it enters any deliverable, factor card, or build decision.

WHEN: synthesizing peer-lane output into a report / spec / factor / plan the owner will act on.

DO: fetch the cited primary source; confirm the attribution (does the cited paper actually say it?); separate the verified MECHANISM from the confident-sounding NUMBER / p-value / label; mark unverifiable figures as such.

DONT: copy a peer lane's "bullshit-detection" table verbatim; assume a skeptical-sounding debunk is safer than a positive claim; cite a number just because the lane stated it confidently.

WHY: 2026-06-14 crypto-playbook round 2 — a Marcel-requested 5-agent primary-source fact-check found 3 of 5 load-bearing claims wrong/overstated, ALL from peer-lane confident negatives: (1) "LLM news-trading is dead, p>0.34" — FALSE, misattributed to FinMem (arXiv 2311.13743) which argues the OPPOSITE; p>0.34 unverifiable; (2) "XGBoost beats deep-learning" cited a source with ZERO ML comparison; it's unsettled, not settled; (3) "maker wins by default" conflated CLOB-making with AMM-LP (LVR). Funding-carry numbers ("36-108%→<10%") were an unsourced mashup (real: carry went NEGATIVE in 2025) and VRP "durable" was contradicted by its own source. The positive claims I verify reflexively; the debunks I had propagated on the lanes' confidence. Both directions need the same gate.
