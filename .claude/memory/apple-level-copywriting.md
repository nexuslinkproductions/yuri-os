RULE: Every sentence should either create desire, reduce doubt, or build conviction.
WHEN: Writing for investors, presentations, product descriptions, marketing copy.
DO: Lead with pain. Make the reader FEEL the inadequacy of the current state. Reveal the solution as a resolution. Use concrete analogies. Simplicity that reveals depth.
DONT: Use defensive framing ("YURI is not..."). Use corporate language. Use filler. Describe when you should make them experience. Say "basic user" — say "everyday user".
STYLE: Think Steve Jobs presenting the iPhone. Not a spec sheet. An experience.
WHY: Marcel explicitly corrected the v1 copywriting as "whacky" and "not Apple-level." The planner's value depends on psychological impact, not feature lists.
