**Why this matters:** Locked design DNA for any future Yuri sprint audit / product page / system showcase. Not "documentation," not "audit," not "report" — **product showcase**. Like an Apple/Linear/Stripe product page, not a changelog.

## Locked operating rules (apply on every Yuri presentation)

1. **Max 15% box content rule** — boxes (cards with borders/frames) may not exceed 15% of visible content. Replace remaining content surfaces with: textures, overlays, gradient washes, backdrop blends, glitch artifacts, torn edges, line-only annotations, edge-bleed sections.

2. **Alternate alignment per section** — don't center-align everything. Rotate left / center / right alignment intentionally per section. Symmetric center = template tell.

3. **Treat the HTML like a video editor timeline** (After Effects / Premiere Pro / DaVinci / Final Cut). Every section is a scene, the scroll is the playhead, transitions are cuts/dissolves/morphs not jumps.

4. **Camera POV is the user** — true 3D scene where the viewer experiences the page as flying through space, not reading a document.

5. **One continuous developing background** — no hard page breaks between sections. Atmospheric layer drifts across the whole page. Section transitions are camera moves not page cuts.

6. **Mouse interaction beyond hover** — drag reveal, grab-and-flip, spin-to-reveal, click-to-disassemble. Cursor is an active instrument.

7. **Japanese graphic design DNA** — Marcel loves Japanese graphic design (super creative, outside the box, text positioning play, asymmetry, intentional negative space, vertical text where it makes sense, dense type clusters paired with vast empty zones).

8. **Mograph + vox-animation aesthetic** — kinetic typography, mass disassembly/reassembly, symbiotic swarm motion, particle morphing, frame-by-frame rebuild quality.

9. **Force-rebound physics in transitions** — animations overshoot then bounce back to place. No hard endings.

10. **Disassemble + reassemble** — title text breaks into pieces during transitions, pieces follow the camera in symbiotic swarm, then morph/transform into the next section's content. The deck is one continuous transforming entity, not 13 slides.

## Standing structural rules

- **Boxes ≤ 15% of visible content** per any visible viewport
- **Hard section cuts banned** — every section transition is a camera move (curve, spiral, dolly, rotate, parallax drift)
- **3 alternate alignment lanes** — left/center/right per section, no consecutive sections sharing alignment
- **Continuous atmospheric layer** — one canvas/scene background that persists across the entire scroll
- **Mouse interaction layer** — every section gets at least one cursor-aware element (magnetic, drag-reveal, hover-explode, click-flip)

## Anti-patterns (instant rejection)
- Centered grids of boxes with same border treatment
- Box-card carousel for product features  
- "Activity monitor" / dashboard-style data presentation (rows of stats)
- Hard cuts between sections
- "Just fades" as transitions
- Consistent box style everywhere
- Centered everything
- "Spec sheet" presentation of anti-patterns / persona

## Reference DNA bank
- **Apple product pages** — image sequence scrubbing, sticky relays, color-temp shifts per section
- **Linear** — restraint, chapter numerals, invisible craft
- **Stripe** — persistent atmospheric layer across sections, camera-move transitions
- **Japanese graphic design** — text positioning play, asymmetry, dense/sparse rhythm
- **Vox / Kurzgesagt / Polygon Pictures** — mograph style, kinetic typography, mass disassembly
- **AE / Blender camera control** — easing curves, dolly mechanics, rebound physics

This is locked. Every Yuri presentation hits this bar minimum.
