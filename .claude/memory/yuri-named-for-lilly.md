YURI is named after Lilly, Marcel's girlfriend. In Japanese, yuri (百合) means lily, so the entire control plane carries her name. It is not a neutral product codename; it is named for someone he loves, and the name should always be treated with that weight. When the work touches naming, tone, or the soul of the system, remember whose name it is.
