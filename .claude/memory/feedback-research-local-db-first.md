RULE: Research starts with OUR OWN local database/corpus FIRST, then escalates to online. We are building a local "online research center" — an indexed knowledge base (FTS5 corpus via `ai search`, GitNexus, _SYSTEM knowledge-base, design packs). Always query local before WebSearch/WebFetch.
WHEN: any research task — design references, technical lookups, copywriting, physics, prior work.
DO: `ai search "<query>"` / scan the indexed corpus + knowledge-base first; THEN go online for what the DB lacks; and INDEX valuable online findings back into the DB so the research center compounds.
DONT: jump straight to WebSearch when the answer (or a strong local reference) may already be in the corpus; leave good research un-indexed.
WHY: Marcel is deliberately building a compounding local research center; local-first is faster, cheaper, private, and grows the asset. He explicitly said "remember that."
