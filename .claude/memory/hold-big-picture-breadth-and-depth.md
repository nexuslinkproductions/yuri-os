RULE: maintain ABSOLUTE breadth AND depth simultaneously — never scope/tunnel in so hard that the complete big picture is lost. Keep a clean, full system map loaded at ALL times, and cross-reference every new piece of work against the whole so it integrates seamlessly: no silos, no drift, no orphan additions that don't add up.

WHEN: every non-trivial design/build/change — ESPECIALLY deep monotropic work on one organ (that's exactly when the system view gets dropped).

DO: (1) before + during deep work, hold the system map loaded — circuitry graph/map, the moat tracks, the math substrate, the sibling organs the work touches; (2) for each increment, check what it touches + its siblings + whether it composes cleanly with what's already there; (3) surface the cross-references explicitly (this connects to X, ripples to Y, must stay consistent with Z); (4) make depth SERVE the whole, never a silo. The circuitry instrument is the EXTERNALIZED big picture that makes holding breadth+depth at once possible — that is its load-bearing job, not decoration.

DONT: tunnel into one mechanism and lose the system view; ship an addition that isn't cross-referenced + integrated; let scope-depth become scope-blindness; treat breadth and depth as a trade-off (Marcel wants BOTH, held via externalization, not one purchased with the other).

RECONCILE with [[feedback-scope-to-the-current-ask]]: that rule governs EXECUTION breadth (build only the current step, don't run ahead into the whole machine); THIS rule governs AWARENESS breadth (hold the complete picture always). Synthesis: **execute narrow, think wide** — do the surgical current step, but always cross-referenced against the full system. They are complementary poles, not a contradiction.

STYLE: this is the META-LAW the [[upgrade-propagation-engine]] (sibling ripple) and [[circuitry-change-propagation-continuity]] (doc/artifact sync) are MECHANISMS OF. Broad-Aware Upgrade is how this law is enforced on every change.

WHY: Marcel's operating reality is twice-exceptional, many threads held at once; the system only stays coherent AND cutting-edge if every deep dive stays cross-referenced to the clean big picture. Siloed/drifted additions = broken coherence = the thing he most needs YURI to prevent (continuity is the product; drift = broken trust).
