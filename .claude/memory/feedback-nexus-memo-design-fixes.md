RULE  The bespoke Nexus Link design system (design-master as METHOD + invent-per-brand, NO HUD) LANDED WELL — Marcel: "the new system worked wonders," seriously impressed. This is the confirmed design direction; keep doing exactly this. Two next-pass refinements he flagged (deferred — apply on the next design iteration, not urgently):
1. LOGO DUPLICATION — the Celtic-knot logo renders TWICE in the memo. Render it ONCE (one hero lockup). Minor but fix it.
2. OVER-BOXING — Marcel is NOT a fan of wrapping every block of text and every visual in a box/card/container. Reduce the boxes: let content breathe, use whitespace + hairline dividers + typographic hierarchy to separate sections; reserve actual cards for genuine callouts (the "do NOT claim" box, key figures), not for everything.

WHEN  Next design pass on the Nexus Link memo or any Nexus Link surface.

DO  One logo instance. Open, breathing layout over boxed-everything. Keep the bespoke palette/type/motion that worked.

DONT  Re-box every paragraph/visual. Don't duplicate the logo. Don't regress to HUD or generic editorial.
