RULE: NO equity shares. NO revenue shares. NO convertible notes. The deal is an ACTIVE GROWING PARTNERSHIP.
WHEN: Structuring investor deals for NEXUS LINK / YURI.
DO: Frame as "your investment flows directly into your own company." NEXUS LINK builds, deploys, maintains YURI infrastructure for the investor. They become the first beneficiary. Investment works twice: NEXUS LINK dev + investor's immediate benefit.
DONT: Offer equity stakes. Offer revenue share. Offer convertible notes/SAFEs. Frame as traditional VC deal.
WHY: Marcel explicitly removed these options. The value proposition is that the investor's own company improves, not that they get shares in something speculative.
