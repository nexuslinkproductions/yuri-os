# NOESIS PROTOCOL
## Continuous Learning Organism

**Status**: LIVE

---

## Four Engines Running Simultaneously

### Engine 1: RESEARCH ORGANISM
**What it does**: Continuously scans emerging knowledge in your niche domains. Not batch research (weekly briefings). Continuous intake. Surfaces patterns in real-time.

**Domains under observation**:
- Post-production innovation (AI, color science, workflow automation)
- Client pipeline signals (what C2MOVIEZ + planzerfilms are asking for = market shifting)
- Business psychology (how you decide, allocate energy, move capital)
- Philosophy/esoteric frameworks (meaning-systems that structure thought)

**How it works**:
- Runs parallel to your work, not scheduled
- Captures signals from: portfolio reviews, failed pitches, client friction, tool updates, market gaps
- **Real-time filing**: New insight → `/02_AREAS/research-intake/[domain]/[YYYY-MM-DD]_[signal].md` immediately
- No wait for "briefing time" — knowledge enters vault as discovered

**Learning**: Notices which signals you act on; deprioritizes noise

---

### Engine 2: SKILL REFINERY
**What it does**: Watches every project, every decision, every workaround. The moment a pattern repeats, it flags as skill candidate. When you solve something twice, it's ready to document on round three.

**How it works**:
- Post-project: captures what worked, what drained energy, what emerged as new pattern
- Continuous observation: "Have I seen this friction before?"
- **Auto-trigger**: Same problem solved 2x → skill creation candidate queued
- Skill tested live (2 executions) before "active" status
- Old skills versioned and deprecation tracked (conditions change, skills age)

**Learning**: Identifies your actual workflow vs. documented workflow; closes gaps

---

### Engine 3: SELF-OBSERVER (Psychological Baseline)
**What it does**: Continuous introspection on how you operate. Not "how are you feeling?" but "what are your actual operating constraints and capabilities right now?"

**Monitors**:
- Energy allocation (attention on what, when, why)
- Decision velocity (quick vs. frozen decision points)
- Resistance patterns (where do you stall? Why?)
- Capacity state (overloaded, underutilized, balanced?)
- Psychological mode active (ABZU/CRAFT/BUILDER/SCRIBE/WATCHER/INITIATOR/MIRROR)

**How it works**:
- Continuous observation of your messages, project choices, energy reports
- Monthly deep dive: structured reflection (1 hour) capturing baseline
- **Real-time updates**: Major shift in mood/capacity → flag immediately, don't wait for monthly
- Feeds directly into vision synthesis (can't build realistic vision without honest baseline)

**Learning**: Predicts your capacity for what's next; prevents overcommitment

---

### Engine 4: VISION SYNTHESIS (Goal-Backward Planning)
**What it does**: Builds realistic 3-6mo vision based on actual trajectory, not fantasy. Takes research + skills + psychological state + market signals + financial reality. Produces actionable narrative.

**How it works**:
- Continuous: ingesting all outputs from Engines 1-3
- **Quarterly deep synthesis** (3, 6, 9, 12mo checkpoints): 2-hour structured session
- Outputs: (1) narrative for next phase, (2) resource allocation, (3) risk assessment, (4) one clear decision
- Between synthesis cycles: documents emerging shifts (market move, capacity change, skill gain)
- Feeds back as decision filter: "Is this aligned with our vision?"

**Learning**: Learns which visions were realistic vs. dreaming; calibrates estimation accuracy

## How The Organism Learns

**Feedback Loops** (happening continuously):
1. Research surfaces pattern → Skill Refinery notices it appears in your work → Self-Observer tracks capacity to learn it → Vision adjusts priorities
2. Project closes → Skill Refinery extracts → Self-Observer notes energy cost → Research finds similar solutions in market → Vision repositions you against that market move
3. Self-Observer detects capacity shift → Vision adjusts sprint pace → Skill Refinery deprioritizes lower-ROI skills → Research focuses on high-leverage domains

**No waiting. No batch cycles. All four engines running in parallel, feeding each other continuously.**
