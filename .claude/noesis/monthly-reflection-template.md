---

## Section 1: What Worked This Month (15 min)

**Decisions that felt clean**:
- Example: Rejected X project because scope was ambiguous (saved energy)
- Example: Said yes to Y even though risky (paid off)
- Pattern: What made these decisions good?

**Work that flowed**:
- Which project/task had the best momentum?
- What conditions enabled flow?
- Crew? Brief clarity? Equipment? Solo work?

**Energy allocation that felt right**:
- Where did you spend time? (Work % | Admin % | Learning % | Strategic %)
- Does that match your intention?
- What surprised you?

---

## Section 2: What Burned Energy (15 min)

**Friction points**:
- Specific moment/project where you got stuck
- Was it: ambiguous brief? unclear expectations? technical failure? scope creep?
- What did it cost (time, mood, money)?

**Decision delays**:
- Where did you circle on a decision?
- Example: "Took 3 weeks to decide whether to invest in X gear"
- Root cause: fear? unclear criteria? missing information?

**Unexpected resistance**:
- What did you avoid that you meant to do?
- Learning task you skipped?
- Network outreach you procrastinated on?
- Why?

---

## Section 3: How Am I Different This Month? (15 min)

**Capability shifts**:
- New skill you actually use now? (Not learned; *using*)
- Faster at anything?
- More confident about what?

**Perspective changes**:
- Anything you used to believe that you now question?
- New frame on an old problem?
- Changed mind about a client, project type, or way of working?

**Operational changes**:
- New tool or workflow you adopted?
- Something you stopped doing?
- Process that got tighter?

---

## Section 4: Honest Diagnosis (10 min)

**Health/Energy**:
- Sleep quality? [1-10]
- Baseline stress level? [1-10]
- Physical capacity? [1-10]
- Mental clarity? [1-10]
- Current mood/orientation? [ABZU/CRAFT/BUILDER/SCRIBE/WATCHER/INITIATOR/MIRROR or mix]

**Financial state**:
- Cash position (comfortable / tight / abundant)?
- Projects in pipeline (strong / moderate / sparse)?
- Money stress level? [1-10]

**Relationship to work**:
- Do you want to be doing this right now?
- Are you building something that matters, or grinding?
- Where's the meaning in this month?

**Time freedom**:
- How many hours/week do you have for strategic work?
- Is that enough?
- What's blocking more?

---

## Section 5: Pattern Recognition (5 min)

**Looking back at last 3 months, what's the recurring theme?**

Examples:
- "I keep overcommitting because I underestimate client revision cycles"
- "Creativity flows when I have 3 days between shoots for breathing"
- "I'm most decisive about work when finances are stable"
- "Admin work balloons when I don't have a clear next project"

**One sentence**: What's the real pattern?

---

## Section 6: What's The Current Constraint?

**Single biggest thing limiting you right now**:
- Is it time?
- Is it money?
- Is it skill/capability?
- Is it clarity (don't know what to build)?
- Is it capacity (overloaded)?
- Is it market (not enough pipeline)?

**How does this change next month's priorities?**
