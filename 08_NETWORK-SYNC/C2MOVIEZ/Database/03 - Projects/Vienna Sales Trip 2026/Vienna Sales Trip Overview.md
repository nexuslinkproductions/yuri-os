## 2. Strategic Summary

### The Competitive Moat

Every competitor in Vienna sells **one thing**: a website OR social media OR video OR IT support. Nobody sells all four as a coordinated package. That is the c2moviez + exeoflow structural advantage.

**The pitch that unlocks everything:**
> "Most agencies will sell you a website. We build the whole digital presence — the website, the content that fills it, the social that drives traffic to it, and the IT infrastructure that keeps it secure. One relationship, one point of accountability."

### The Vienna Opportunity

Vienna's established SME market (10-150 employees, EUR 300K-5M revenue) has a structural gap:
- Websites built 5-10 years ago
- Zero or inconsistent social media presence
- No in-house video production
- Manual or insecure IT infrastructure

These are not failing companies. They have real revenue, real teams, real budgets. They just have not been shown what the gap is costing them.

### Service Demand Across 40 Targets

| Service | Demand | Role |
|---------|--------|------|
| Video / Content | **38/40** | Universal entry point |
| Social Media | **35/40** | Immediate upsell after video |
| Website Rebuild | **28/40** | High-value project work |
| Ad Management | **25/40** | Recurring revenue driver |
| IT / Cybersecurity | **22/40** | exeoflow differentiator |
| Motion / Design | **18/40** | Premium add-on |

**Strategy:** Start with video (universal need). Layer social. Upsell IT for data-sensitive clients (dental, finance, security). Lock in full-service retainers.
