# Partnership Agreement

## c2moviez GmbH x SILASWIRTH

---

## 1. Preamble

This Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**Party A — c2moviez GmbH**
Represented by Claudio Tinner (CEO / Creative Director)
Swiss creative technology firm — film, content production, web development, IT services
Based in Thurgau, Switzerland

**Party B — Silas Wirth ("SILASWIRTH")**
Motion Designer & Creative Partner
Freelance creative professional specializing in motion graphics, text animation, and brand motion design

**Effective Date:** [TO BE AGREED]

**Purpose:** This Agreement establishes the terms of a strategic creative partnership under which Silas Wirth operates as a dedicated creative partner of c2moviez GmbH. Both parties bring complementary capabilities to serve premium clients in the real estate, luxury property, and marine sectors, and jointly pursue new business in these verticals.

**Background:** This Agreement reflects the decisions made at the partnership dinner on 16 April 2026 (Molino, Frauenfeld), where both parties committed to building a strong partnership under the c2moviez brand, with a clear niche focus and transparent compensation structures.

---

## 2. Partnership Structure

### 2.1 Relationship

Silas Wirth joins c2moviez GmbH as a **creative partner**, not as an employee. Silas operates as a freelance professional under a dedicated partnership arrangement with c2moviez. This is a commercial collaboration, not an employment relationship. No employer/employee obligations (social insurance, holiday pay, etc.) arise from this Agreement.

### 2.2 Operating Model

- All client-facing work is delivered **under the c2moviez brand**. Silas is presented to clients as part of the c2moviez creative team.
- c2moviez GmbH handles all client invoicing. Silas invoices c2moviez GmbH for his services according to the compensation model defined in Section 4.
- Silas retains the right to operate under his own brand (SILASWIRTH) for personal projects and clients outside the scope of this Agreement, subject to the exclusivity terms in Section 7.

### 2.3 Client Ownership

- All client relationships acquired or serviced under this partnership belong to c2moviez GmbH.
- Silas has direct client contact when operationally necessary (briefings, feedback rounds, on-set coordination), but c2moviez remains the contractual partner for all clients.

## 3. Niche Focus

### 3.1 Target Verticals

The partnership is strategically focused on the following premium verticals:

1. **Real Estate** — luxury properties, developments, architectural showcases
2. **Luxury Goods** — high-end brands, premium lifestyle products
3. **Marine / Boats** — yacht content, boat manufacturers, marine lifestyle (building on existing GANZ BOATS AG relationship)

### 3.2 Service Positioning

The partnership positions c2moviez as the premium creative production house for these verticals in the Swiss and DACH market. A dedicated **high-end landing page** will be built to push sales specifically in the real estate/luxury niche, featuring:

- Portfolio of real estate / luxury / marine work
- Specialized service packages (see Section 6)
- Lead capture and sales funnel
- Combined team showcase (c2moviez + Silas + NexusLinkProductions)

### 3.3 Other Verticals

While the niche focus guides marketing and sales strategy, the partnership applies to all c2moviez client work where motion graphics, text animation, or related services are required. Silas may be engaged on any c2moviez project at the discretion of both parties.

## 4. Compensation Model

### 4.1 Client Acquisition Commission

When either party brings in a new client that results in a signed engagement:

| Scenario | Commission |
|----------|-----------|
| **Silas brings a new client** | [PROPOSED: 15% of net revenue from that client in the first 12 months] |
| **Claudio/c2moviez brings a new client** (Silas delivers work) | Standard per-deliverable rates apply (Section 4.2) |
| **Joint acquisition** (both parties actively involved in winning the client) | [PROPOSED: 10% commission to the non-lead party on first 12 months] |

**Conditions:**
- "Bringing a client" means making the initial introduction that leads to a signed engagement. Passive referrals (e.g., "I know someone") do not qualify unless they result in a direct meeting.
- Commission is calculated on net revenue (after direct third-party costs but before internal costs).
- Commission is paid out within 30 days of c2moviez receiving payment from the client.
- After the 12-month commission period, the client relationship belongs to c2moviez and standard rates apply for ongoing work.

### 4.2 Per-Deliverable Rate Schedule

Silas invoices c2moviez GmbH for completed deliverables at the following rates:

| Deliverable Type | Unit | Rate |
|-----------------|------|------|
| **Motion Graphics** (general) | Per day (8h) | [PROPOSED: CHF 850/day] |
| **Text Animation / Kinetic Typography** | Per day (8h) | [PROPOSED: CHF 850/day] |
| **Brand Motion Design** (logo animations, visual identity motion systems) | Per project | [PROPOSED: CHF 1,200 - 3,000 depending on scope] |
| **Product Demo Animations** | Per project | [PROPOSED: CHF 1,500 - 4,000 depending on complexity] |
| **Social Media Motion Content** (reels, stories, short-form) | Per piece | [PROPOSED: CHF 300 - 600 per piece] |
| **Pitch Deck Motion Design** | Per deck | [PROPOSED: CHF 800 - 2,000 depending on slides/complexity] |
| **Hourly Rate** (for smaller tasks, revisions, ad-hoc work) | Per hour | [PROPOSED: CHF 110/hour] |

**Notes on rates:**
- All rates are net (excluding VAT where applicable).
- These rates reflect the startup phase of the partnership. Both parties acknowledge that rates will be reviewed and adjusted as the business grows.
- For complex projects, a fixed project fee is agreed in writing before work begins. The project fee is based on estimated hours at the day rate.
- Rates are reviewed every 6 months (see Section 4.5).

### 4.3 Revenue Split on Joint Projects

For projects where both Claudio and Silas contribute creative work (e.g., Claudio shoots video, Silas creates motion overlays):

| Model | Split |
|-------|-------|
| **Standard joint project** | [PROPOSED: 60% c2moviez / 40% Silas of net project revenue] |
| **Silas-led project** (Silas is primary creative, c2moviez provides client management + infrastructure) | [PROPOSED: 50% c2moviez / 50% Silas of net project revenue] |
| **c2moviez-led project** (Claudio is primary creative, Silas provides supporting motion work) | Per-deliverable rate (Section 4.2) — no revenue split |

**How "joint project" is defined:**
- A project qualifies as joint when both parties contribute significant creative work (not just project management or minor revisions).
- The split model (standard vs. Silas-led) is agreed before project start and documented in the project brief.
- The c2moviez share covers: client management, sales, invoicing, infrastructure, brand, tools, and Claudio's creative contribution.
- Direct third-party costs (equipment rental, travel, software licenses) are deducted before the split.

### 4.4 Payment Terms

- Silas submits invoices to c2moviez GmbH monthly (or per-project for larger engagements).
- Payment terms: **30 days net** from invoice date.
- If c2moviez has not yet been paid by the end client, payment to Silas may be deferred by mutual agreement, but not beyond 60 days from invoice date.
- All payments in CHF.

### 4.5 Rate Review

- Rates and splits are reviewed every **6 months** from the effective date of this Agreement.
- Either party may propose adjustments based on market conditions, volume of work, and business performance.
- Changes require written agreement from both parties.

## 5. Team Synergy — c2moviez + SILASWIRTH + NexusLinkProductions

### 5.1 Three-Way Creative Force

This partnership operates within a broader creative alliance:

| Partner | Role | Base |
|---------|------|------|
| **c2moviez GmbH** (Claudio Tinner) | CEO, Creative Director, Videographer, Client Management, Sales, Web, IT | Thurgau, Switzerland |
| **SILASWIRTH** (Silas Wirth) | Motion Designer, Text Animation, Brand Motion, Visual Identity | Switzerland |
| **NexusLinkProductions** (Marcel Spatz) | Post-Production Lead, Video Production, Motion Graphics, Austria Branch Manager | Vienna, Austria |

### 5.2 How We Work Together

- **Client intake & sales:** Claudio (c2moviez) leads client relationships and sales. Silas and Marcel are looped in for scoping when their skills are needed.
- **Production:** Claudio handles videography, photography, and on-location shoots. Silas handles motion graphics, text animation, and brand motion. Marcel handles post-production, editing, color grading, and additional motion work.
- **Delivery pipeline:** c2moviez shoots/captures --> Marcel edits/grades --> Silas adds motion/animation --> Claudio reviews/delivers to client. (Flexible per project.)
- **Capacity planning:** Projects are assigned based on availability, skillset, and client needs. No one is obligated to accept work beyond their capacity.

### 5.3 NexusLinkProductions Relationship

Marcel Spatz / NexusLinkProductions operates under a separate partnership agreement with c2moviez GmbH. The billing model is consistent: c2moviez invoices the client, NexusLinkProductions invoices c2moviez for its share. This Agreement does not govern the c2moviez-NexusLink relationship, but both parties acknowledge the three-way synergy as core to the business strategy.

### 5.4 Combined Market Position

Together, the three partners offer:
- Full-service video production (shoot to final delivery)
- Motion graphics and animation at every level
- Post-production and color grading
- Web development and digital presence
- Coverage across Switzerland and Austria (DACH expansion)
- Premium quality at competitive rates — lean team, no overhead bloat

## 6. Scope of Services

### 6.1 What Silas Delivers

Under this partnership, Silas provides the following services to c2moviez clients:

1. **Motion Graphics** — animated visual content for film, web, and social media
2. **Text Animation & Kinetic Typography** — animated text sequences for corporate videos, social content, and brand films
3. **Brand Motion Design** — animated logos, visual identity motion systems, brand intros/outros
4. **Product Demo Animations** — animated product showcases, feature visualizations, explainer animations
5. **Pitch Deck Motion Design** — animated presentations for sales and investor contexts
6. **Social Media Motion Content** — short-form animated content for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok
7. **Graphic Design Support** — static visual design when required as part of a larger motion project

### 6.2 What c2moviez Provides

c2moviez GmbH provides the following to support the partnership:

1. **Client pipeline** — existing and new clients across all verticals
2. **Brand & reputation** — Silas operates under the established c2moviez brand
3. **Sales & client management** — Claudio handles all sales, proposals, contracts, and client communications
4. **Invoicing & administration** — c2moviez handles all financial administration with clients
5. **Infrastructure** — project management (Plane.so), communication tools, file sharing, operational systems
6. **Marketing** — c2moviez markets the partnership services, including the dedicated landing page
7. **Creative direction** — Claudio provides creative direction and quality control on deliverables

### 6.3 Quality Standards

- All deliverables must meet c2moviez quality standards as determined by Claudio (Creative Director).
- Revision rounds are included as part of the project scope (typically 2 rounds of revisions are standard).
- If a deliverable requires rework beyond the agreed revision rounds due to quality issues on Silas's side, the additional work is at Silas's cost. If rework is due to client changes or c2moviez brief changes, it is compensated at the hourly rate.

## 7. Exclusivity & Non-Compete

### 7.1 Niche Exclusivity

During the term of this Agreement, Silas agrees to work exclusively through c2moviez for clients in the following verticals:

- Real estate (luxury and commercial)
- Marine / boats
- Luxury goods and lifestyle

This means: if a real estate, boat, or luxury client approaches Silas directly, Silas routes them to c2moviez. In return, c2moviez commits to engaging Silas on all motion graphics work within these verticals.

### 7.2 General Non-Compete

Outside the exclusive verticals, Silas is free to take on freelance work under his own brand (SILASWIRTH), provided:

- It does not conflict with active c2moviez projects (timeline or client overlap).
- It does not involve clients currently served by c2moviez.
- Silas communicates openly about his external workload to ensure capacity planning works for both sides.

### 7.3 Non-Solicitation

For the duration of the Agreement and **12 months after termination**, neither party shall:

- Directly solicit or contract with clients introduced by the other party through this partnership.
- Recruit or solicit team members or partners of the other party.

## 8. Intellectual Property & Brand Usage

### 8.1 Work Product

All work produced under this partnership for c2moviez clients is the intellectual property of c2moviez GmbH (and is licensed/transferred to the respective client as per c2moviez's client agreements).

Silas grants c2moviez full usage rights to all deliverables created under this Agreement.

### 8.2 Portfolio Rights

Silas has the right to use work created under this partnership in his personal portfolio (SILASWIRTH), provided:

- The client has not requested confidentiality.
- The work is credited as "created for c2moviez GmbH" or "c2moviez x SILASWIRTH."
- Portfolio usage begins no earlier than 3 months after delivery to the client (or upon client publication, whichever comes first).

### 8.3 Brand Usage

- Silas may identify himself as a "c2moviez Creative Partner" on his website and social media.
- Silas may use the c2moviez logo in the context of the partnership (with approval for each specific use).
- c2moviez may feature Silas and his work on the c2moviez website, landing page, and marketing materials.
- Neither party may make binding commitments on behalf of the other without prior written consent.

### 8.4 Pre-Existing IP

Any tools, templates, techniques, or assets that Silas brings into the partnership that were created before this Agreement remain Silas's property. Silas grants c2moviez a non-exclusive license to use these in client work during the term of the Agreement.

## 9. Term & Termination

### 9.1 Trial Period

The first **3 months** from the effective date constitute a trial period. During this period:

- Either party may terminate with **14 days written notice**.
- All compensation for work completed up to the termination date is paid in full.
- Both parties commit to completing any in-progress client deliverables before the termination takes effect (or arranging a clean handover).

### 9.2 Term

After the trial period, this Agreement continues on an **indefinite basis** until terminated by either party.

### 9.3 Termination After Trial

Either party may terminate the Agreement with **3 months written notice** (to the end of a calendar month).

### 9.4 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate immediately in writing if:

- The other party commits a material breach of this Agreement and does not remedy it within 14 days of written notice.
- The other party becomes insolvent or enters bankruptcy proceedings.
- The other party engages in conduct that damages the reputation of the partnership or the other party's brand.

### 9.5 Post-Termination

Upon termination:

- All outstanding payments are settled within 30 days.
- Client acquisition commissions for the 12-month commission period continue to be paid even after termination, for clients introduced before the termination date.
- The non-solicitation clause (Section 7.3) remains in effect for 12 months.
- Silas returns or deletes any confidential c2moviez materials within 14 days.
- Portfolio rights (Section 8.2) survive termination for work already completed.

## 10. Confidentiality

### 10.1 Obligations

Both parties agree to treat as confidential:

- Client information, contracts, and pricing
- Internal business data (revenue, costs, margins, strategy)
- Proprietary tools, processes, and systems
- Any information explicitly marked as confidential

### 10.2 Exceptions

Confidentiality does not apply to information that:

- Is publicly available through no fault of the receiving party
- Was already known to the receiving party before disclosure
- Is independently developed without reference to confidential information
- Must be disclosed by law or regulatory requirement

### 10.3 Duration

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for **2 years**.
