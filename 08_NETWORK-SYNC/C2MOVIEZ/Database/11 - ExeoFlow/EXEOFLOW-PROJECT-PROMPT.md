# ExeoFlow × c2moviez Partnership Project

## Project Overview

This document outlines the strategic partnership between **c2moviez GmbH** (represented by Claudio Tinner) and **ExeoFlow** (represented by Raphael) to drive growth, user acquisition, and brand development for the all-in-one business operating system.

---

## About the Parties

### c2moviez GmbH
- **Founder & Lead**: Claudio Tinner
- **Expertise**: Content creation, video production, graphic design, marketing strategy, sales acceleration, and training/education
- **Scope**: Full-service growth and brand development partner
- **Location**: Switzerland (DACH region focus)

### ExeoFlow
- **Founder & Product Lead**: Raphael
- **Product**: All-in-one business operating system combining CRM, invoicing, time tracking, expense management, smart writing, accounting, integrations (Plane.so API), and self-hosted options
- **Stage**: Growth-focused, seeking market penetration and brand awareness
- **Target Markets**: DACH region and European SMBs/consultancies

## Partnership Tracks

### Track A: Join ExeoFlow (Co-Founder Model)
**Structure**: Equity-based partnership

**Key Terms**:
- c2moviez becomes co-founder with equity stake
- Joint strategic decision-making and governance
- Revenue share arrangement and upside participation
- Unified brand identity (services become internal)
- Long-term commitment and alignment
- Higher risk, higher reward model

**Best For**: If c2moviez wants deep integration and long-term equity upside

---

### Track B: External Partner (Default Model)
**Structure**: Commission-based partnership

**Key Terms**:
- c2moviez operates as independent external partner
- Remuneration provided per subscription basis
- Separate legal entities maintained
- Quarterly partnership reporting and reviews
- Flexible engagement model
- Lower risk, recurring revenue model

**Best For**: If c2moviez wants flexibility with recurring commission income

## Key Conditions to Negotiate

### 01. Commission Percentage
- Negotiable base rate applied to all referred subscription revenue
- Tiered escalations based on volume and performance
- Review annually for market competitiveness

### 02. Client Attribution
- Clear attribution rules in CRM system
- c2moviez must be explicitly credited as source
- Pre-existing customers marked separately
- Dispute resolution process (14 days)

### 03. Payout Schedule
- Monthly or quarterly settlement options
- Currency: CHF, EUR, or USD
- Bank transfer method
- Minimum threshold (if any): _____

### 04. Minimum Commitments
- Quarterly deliverable targets for content, design, sales
- Example targets (to be set):
  - _____ content pieces per quarter
  - _____ design deliverables per quarter
  - _____ qualified leads per quarter
- 48-hour response time on ExeoFlow requests
- Two-quarter review clause with adjustment option

### 05. Exclusivity Clause
- No promotion of competing all-in-one platforms during term
- "Competing" defined as: CRM + Invoicing + Time Tracking + Financials in single platform
- Carve-outs: c2moviez core services, pre-existing clients (disclosed)
- 12-month tail period applies post-termination

### 06. IP & Content Ownership
- **ExeoFlow Owns**: Marketing content, videos, designs, copy
- **c2moviez Retains**: Portfolio rights, case study rights
- **Raphael Owns**: All product IP, ExeoFlow platform
- **Trademark Use**: Requires written consent

### 07. Contract Duration & Exit
- **Initial Term**: 12 months
- **Auto-Renewal**: Successive 12-month terms unless 60-day non-renewal notice
- **Termination for Convenience**: 90 days notice
- **Termination for Cause**: Material breach + 30-day cure period
- **Tail Period**: 12 months commission on active customers post-termination

### 08. Liability & Data
- **Liability Cap**: 12 months of commissions paid/payable
- **Exclusions**: No indirect, incidental, or consequential damages
- **GDPR/DSG**: DPA execution, 48-hour data forwarding, standard security measures
- **Compliance**: Each party responsible for own obligations

### 09. Confidentiality & NDA
- 24-month survival post-termination
- Standard exceptions: public domain, independent development
- Injunctive relief for breaches
- Third-party sub-processor agreements if needed

### 10. Dispute Resolution
- **Step 1**: 30-day negotiation period
- **Step 2**: Mediation under Swiss rules
- **Step 3**: Arbitration under Swiss law (single or three arbitrators based on amount)
- **Governing Law**: Swiss law applies

## Design Direction

### Visual Identity

**Color Palette**:
- Primary: `#7B5CFF` (Purple)
- Secondary: `#00C8FF` (Cyan)
- Background: `#FFFFFF` (White)
- Text: `#1A1A1A` (Dark Gray)
- Accent: `#55BDE7` (Blue)

**Typography**:
- Font Family: Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI
- Weights: 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-bold), 700 (Bold)
- Sizes: Responsive, mobile-first scaling

**Design System**:
- Glassmorphism: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` glass cards
- Backdrop blur: 10-20px
- Border radius: 12-16px
- Shadows: Subtle gradient orbs, soft elevation
- Animations: Fade-in on scroll, smooth transitions

**Components**:
- Fixed frosted glass navigation
- Hero section with gradient text
- Grid layouts (4-col, 2-col, 8-col responsive)
- Feature chips with gradient icons
- Track cards with badges
- Condition cards numbered 01-08
- Provision card with negotiable percentage
