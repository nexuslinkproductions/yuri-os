### Track A — Co-Founder / Equity Partner
- Claudio joins ExeoFlow as co-founder alongside Raphael

- c2moviez operates as independent external partner
- Recurring provision (commission %) per ExeoFlow subscription sold

- Quarterly reporting & transparent CRM tracking
