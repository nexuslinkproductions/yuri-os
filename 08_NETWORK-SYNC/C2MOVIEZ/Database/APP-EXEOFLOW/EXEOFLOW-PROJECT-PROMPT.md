## Context

**c2moviez GmbH** (Claudio Tinner, Founder) — a Swiss-based full-service creative studio offering cinematic videography, FPV drone, photography, web design & development, content creation, and social media production. Website: www.c2moviez.com

**ExeoFlow** (Raphael, Founder & Developer) — a self-deployed, all-in-one business operating system. Features: CRM & client management, invoicing & billing, time tracking, expense management, Smart Writer (transcribe, translate, summarize), accounting & reporting, and API integrations (linked to Plane.so). Website: www.exeoflow.com

**Raphael** is the technical partner of c2moviez who deploys apps. ExeoFlow is already used internally at c2moviez as the operational brain — connected to Plane.so (project/ticket management) via API and surfaced through ops.c2moviez.com (the COO assistant dashboard).

## The Partnership

Claudio / c2moviez will be responsible for **all go-to-market activities** for ExeoFlow:

1. **Content Creation & Marketing** — Video production, social media, ad campaigns, email sequences, marketing funnels, blog posts, case studies
2. **Graphic Design & Branding** — UI/UX design support, brand identity, visual assets, pitch decks, product screenshots, marketing collateral, demo videos
3. **Sales & Client Acquisition** — Lead generation, product demos, sales presentations, client onboarding, CRM pipeline management, conversion optimization (focus: DACH market)
4. **Training & Schooling** — Client onboarding workshops, tutorial videos, documentation, feature training, train-the-trainer sessions, knowledge base content

---

## Partnership Structure — Two Tracks

### Track A: Join ExeoFlow (Co-Founder / Equity Partner)
- Claudio joins ExeoFlow as co-founder or equity partner alongside Raphael
- Joint decision-making on product & go-to-market
- Revenue share + equity appreciation
- Brand: "ExeoFlow — by Raphael & Claudio"
- All services become internal contributions

### Track B: External Strategic Partner (Provision-Based)
- c2moviez operates as independent external partner
- Recurring provision (commission %) per ExeoFlow subscription sold
- Commission applies for the lifetime of each referred client's subscription
- Clean contractual separation between entities
- Quarterly reporting & transparent tracking

**Default:** Until a track is formally selected, Track B terms apply.

---

## Provision Model (Track B)

- **Type:** Recurring % per subscription per month
- **Duration:** Lifetime of the client's active subscription
- **Attribution:** CRM tagging in ExeoFlow, referral codes, or demo-to-close tracking
- **Upsells:** Same % applies to upgrades
- **Tail period:** Commission continues for X months after contract termination on existing clients
- **Tiered rates possible:** Higher % for first 50 clients (Launch), standard for 51-200 (Growth), volume rate for 200+ (Scale)

---

## Key Contract Conditions to Negotiate

1. **Commission percentage** — exact recurring % (consider tiered)
2. **Client attribution rules** — CRM tagging, who counts as "referred"
3. **Payout schedule** — monthly/quarterly, net days, currency (CHF/EUR)
4. **Minimum commitments** — quarterly deliverables (posts, demos, onboardings, campaigns)
5. **Exclusivity / non-compete** — no competing all-in-one platforms during partnership
6. **IP & content ownership** — ExeoFlow owns marketing content; c2moviez retains portfolio rights
7. **Confidentiality / NDA** — 24-month survival after termination
8. **Data protection** — GDPR / Swiss DSG compliance, DPA if needed
9. **Contract term** — 12 months initial, auto-renewal, 90 days termination notice
10. **Dispute resolution** — negotiation → mediation → arbitration under Swiss law
