SHIPSTER Campaign Plan

"Dein Sommer. Dein Boot. Dein Moment."

Brand: SHIPSTER — Boat Sharing (Swiss Lakes)
Target: Boat license holders in Switzerland (niche, high-intent)
Format: 9:16 vertical, multi-platform master
Assets: SHIPSTER logo (teal/gold) + real boat/lake footage (user-provided)



Tier 1 — Hero & Emotional (Cinematic, Premium)

1. "Dein Sommer. Dein Boot. Dein Moment." — Hero Video (15s)





Type: Campaign anchor film



Energy: Dynamic — purposeful, building momentum



Concept: Golden-hour lake sequence. Water, boat, freedom. Three beats matching the three phrases. Logo resolve at end.



Music: Warm electronic build, Swiss summer feel

2. "Freiheit hat eine Adresse: das Wasser." — Cinematic Video (10s)





Type: Emotional/aspirational



Energy: Calm — ambient drift, wide shots



Concept: Slow cinematic panning across still water at dawn. Single boat gliding. Text fades in at the end. Pure mood.

3. "Das Boot gehört Dir — für heute." — Short Video (8s)





Type: Ownership feeling



Energy: Calm to Dynamic — builds



Concept: POV from the helm. Hands on wheel, wake behind, open water ahead. One-day king energy.



Tier 2 — Urgency & Convenience (Punchy, Fast)

4. "5 Minuten bis zum Ablegen." — Short Video (8s)





Type: Speed/urgency



Energy: Dynamic — quick cuts



Concept: Countdown energy. Phone → app → dock → untie → go. Five-minute promise made visual.

5. "Spontan. Jederzeit. Überall." — Montage (8s)





Type: Multi-location quick-cut



Energy: Dynamic — fast pacing (1-2s per shot)



Concept: Three different lakes, three different moments, same ease. Rapid cuts. Each word = one shot.

6. "3 Klicks. Abfahrt." — Motion Graphic + Live (8s)





Type: UX demo feel



Energy: Dynamic



Concept: Stylized app interaction (3 taps) → hard cut to real boat departure. Digital to physical in seconds.

7. "Bereit für Dich." — Short Video (6s)





Type: Product-ready reveal



Energy: Calm — clean, confident



Concept: Empty boat at dock, pristine, waiting. Camera drifts toward it. "Ready for you." Simple, inviting.



Tier 3 — Hype & Lifestyle (High Energy)

8. "Full Send Schweizer Seen Edition" — Hype Video (10s)





Type: Action/energy



Energy: Aggressive — fast cuts, kinetic



Concept: Wake spray, speed shots, group energy on deck, whip pans between lake moments. Full send energy. This one breaks the premium tone intentionally.

9. "Kein Stress. Nur See." — Contrast Video (8s)





Type: Anti-hype counterpoint



Energy: Calm — stillness, silence



Concept: City stress montage (fast, loud) → hard cut to silent lake, drifting boat. Instant calm. The contrast IS the message.

10. "3'500 Credits. Unendlich Sommer." — Motion Graphic (8s)





Type: Pricing CTA



Energy: Dynamic



Concept: Credits counter animates up → transforms into summer montage → end card with pricing and CTA. The value proposition piece.



Production Approach





Upload reference footage — Real boat/lake photos and videos to canvas



Generate per tier — Start with Tier 1 hero, set visual direction, then batch Tier 2 + 3



Music first — Generate tracks per energy tier, beat-align shot timing



Keyframes from references — Use real footage as base, generate cinematic keyframes



Assemble with text overlays — German headlines, SHIPSTER branding, CTA end cards



Platform adapt — Master 9:16, then reframe for other formats as needed



Brand Rules





Logo: SHIPSTER shield — small, persistent lower corner or end card



Colors: Teal + gold from brand, warm summer tones for footage



Typography: Clean sans-serif, high contrast for mobile readability



Language: German (Swiss market)



CTA: App download / "Jetzt buchen" on final frames
