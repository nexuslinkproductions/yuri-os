## Austrian legal/tax spine — you can start now ✅ (WKO/SVS primary sources)
- **No compulsory SVS social insurance** until self-employment income exceeds **€6,613.20/year (2026)**. Register with SVS within ~1 month of *starting* if you expect to exceed it.
- Once over: **18.5% pension + 6.8% health = 25.3%** + 1.53% Selbständigenvorsorge + ~€12.96/mo accident. First 3 years billed *provisionally* (min base ~€551/mo), then reconciled.
- **Kleinunternehmer VAT exemption to €55,000 gross** (current + prior year; raised from €35k net on 2025-01-01).
- EU cross-border threshold **€100,000** (EU-27 only; US/third-country out of scope).
- **EU B2B = reverse-charge:** you invoice EU business clients **without** Austrian VAT — BUT you must obtain a **UID number** (even while VAT-exempt), verify the client's UID (VIES), and file the Zusammenfassende Meldung (FinanzOnline). **Get the UID early.**
