# The Comprehensive AI Service Roster — 2-Person Claude-Powered Unit (2026)

**Operating reality first.** You (Marcel + Mike) are not "freelancers with a laptop." You are an expert operator + onboarding operator wrapped around a stack that does 80%+ of the production. That changes which categories are good for you: anything where the bottleneck is *production capacity* is your friend; anything where the bottleneck is *trust/reviews* (cold-start marketplaces) is your enemy in week 1. The whole roster is sorted to exploit that asymmetry.

**Read this before the table dump — it's the thing that unlocks everything else:**

## The Fiverr confusion, settled

You're not crazy. Fiverr genuinely has no "jobs to apply for" in the normal sense. Here's the actual split:

- **Fiverr = a store shelf (gig marketplace).** You list a *product* ("I will build you an n8n automation for $250"), buyers browse and buy it off the shelf. You do NOT apply to anything. There's no real job feed for new sellers — the old "Buyer Requests" feed is effectively dead, replaced by an AI brief-matching system that only surfaces you once you've earned a Success Score 7+. So as a fresh account you are invisible furniture until you manufacture your first 1–3 reviews. [source: fiverr.com; upwork.com]
- **Upwork = a job board (post-and-bid).** Clients *post jobs*, you send proposals/bids, they interview and hire. This is the "apply to jobs" model you were looking for. [source: upwork.com; fiverr.com]
- **Also apply-to-jobs:** Freelancer.com, PeoplePerHour, Guru, Workana, Wellfound, Contra Opportunities, and AI-native boards (Mercor, etc.). [source: bestjobsearchapps.com]
- **Toptal / Gun.io / Braintrust** = vetted networks; you apply to *the network* once (3–8 wk gate), then get matched. Zero month-1 cash by design.

**Strategic consequence:** Week 1 cash comes from **Upwork (apply to real jobs) + direct/productized outreach (you create the demand)**, NOT Fiverr. Fiverr is a *month 2–3 review-farming and inbound* play, not a launch surface. Don't waste week 1 polishing Fiverr gigs that no one will see.

**One adversarial flag before the roster:** every "month 1 = $X" number below is a *capacity ceiling assuming you win the trust game*, not a floor. The stack removes your production constraint; it does **not** remove the first-review constraint on marketplaces or the outreach-volume constraint on direct channels. The single highest-leverage thing you can do in week 1 is **manufacture proof** — 2–3 cheap/fast/overdelivered jobs to bank reviews, plus a portfolio of self-made demo artifacts (a real automation, a real explainer video, a real RAG bot) you can show *before* anyone hires you. Skill is not your gap. Proof-of-skill-to-strangers is.

## TIER A — Start-This-Week Cash (fast scope, low trust gate, you can self-produce proof today)

These win because they're (a) deliverable in 24–72h, (b) easy to overdeliver for a first review, and (c) Marcel can self-produce a portfolio sample *tonight*.

### A1. Short-Form Video Repurposing (long-form → Reels/Shorts/TikTok batches)
- **What it is:** Client gives you a podcast/webinar/YouTube long-form; you cut 8–20 vertical clips/month with captions, hooks, b-roll, motion. AI does clip-selection/transcription/draft captions (OpusClip-class), Marcel does the pacing/craft finish that separates it from raw AI slop.
- **Best platforms:** Direct/r/forhire + Upwork + Fiverr (later). This is *the* productized-subscription darling on r/forhire right now. [source: feedbird.com; shortvids.co]
- **Price (2026):** $100–$400 per clip à la carte; **subscription $99/mo entry → $1,350–$1,500/mo typical → $4,999/mo agency tier.** [source: shortvids.co; feedbird.com; lyfemarketing.com]
- **Demand:** Very high (parent skill +329% YoY). **Claude automation: ~70%** (clip-pick, transcript, caption draft, repurpose copy; Marcel finishes). **Time-to-deliver:** 24–48h per batch. **Setup:** Marcel already has this — Premiere/AE/Resolve + OpusClip-class tool. **Money-now: 9/10.**

### A2. Landing Page / Microsite Build + Stripe Wiring (fixed-scope product)
- **What it is:** One-page or small site, copy + design + responsive build + Stripe/Calendly wiring. Sold as a *fixed product*, not hourly.
- **Best platforms:** Upwork (huge perennial demand) + direct + Fiverr later.
- **Price (2026):** **$800–$3,000 one-off**, $1,000–$5,000 for sales pages; upsell to maintenance. [source: index.dev; sidestackers.com]
- **Demand:** Perennial-high. **Claude automation: ~85%** (Claude+Codex ship the Next.js/React/HTML end-to-end; you direct design + QA). **Time-to-deliver:** 1–3 days. **Setup:** deploy target (Vercel/Netlify), Stripe acct, a design eye (Marcel has it). **Money-now: 9/10.**

### A3. AI-Assisted SEO / GEO Content Packages (human-edited, niched)
- **What it is:** "10–20 AI-drafted, human-edited, GEO-optimized posts/mo." The 2026 twist is **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — structuring content to get cited by AI search, which clients now pay a premium for.
- **Best platforms:** Upwork + direct retainer + lower-tier boards.
- **Price (2026):** Per-article mid-tier **$150–$375**; **GEO/AEO retainers $1,500–$6,000/mo** (full-stack AEO+GEO+LLM-seeding hits $12k+). [source: bestwriting.com; fuelonline.com; stackmatix.com]
- **Demand:** High and *shifting toward GEO* (your differentiator vs. commodity writers). **Claude automation: ~80%** (draft + structure; human edit for originality/ToS). **Time-to-deliver:** 24–72h per batch. **Setup:** an SEO/GEO checklist, one niche to own. **Money-now: 8/10.** *(ToS note: never ship raw output — Upwork/Fiverr both ban undisclosed raw-AI resale; "AI-drafted, expert-edited" is the compliant, higher-margin frame.)*

### A4. Pitch-Deck / Report / Presentation Design (styled HTML→PDF, your `yuri-report` lane)
- **What it is:** Investor decks, sales decks, styled reports. Your stack already produces styled-HTML→PDF deliverables natively.
- **Best platforms:** Upwork + direct + Fiverr.
- **Price (2026):** **$100–$150/slide freelance**; typical deck **$1,000–$5,000** (12–15 slides, 3–4 revisions). [source: spectup.com; whitepage.studio]
- **Demand:** Steady. AI tools (Gamma/Tome) compress the commodity floor — your edge is *narrative + motion + real design taste*, not template fills. **Claude automation: ~75%.** **Time-to-deliver:** 1–2 days. **Setup:** design system (you have one), PDF render pipeline (you have it). **Money-now: 7/10.**

### A5. Document Processing / PDF → Structured Data (one-off jobs)
- **What it is:** "Turn these 500 invoices/contracts/PDFs into clean JSON/CSV/sheet." Your MinerU stack productized.
- **Best platforms:** Upwork + lower-tier boards (buyers here are desperate and few sellers deliver clean).
- **Price (2026):** One-off jobs **$150–$1,500** depending on volume/messiness; reference commercial tools sit at $39–$500/mo so you price *under tool+setup pain* but above DIY. [source: parseur.com; klippa.com]

## The compliance spine (applies to every category — this is what keeps you un-banned)

1. **Disclose AI when asked. Never lie.** "Yes, I use AI tools as part of my expert workflow" is the universal safe answer and increasingly a *selling point*. [source: help.fiverr.com; support.upwork.com]
2. **Never resell raw AI output ("profit arbitrage") — Fiverr bans it explicitly, Upwork calls it misrepresentation.** Always add custom config / QA / integration / craft. This is also where your margin lives.
3. **Own the rights; comply with each tool's ToS for commercial resale.**
4. **No deepfakes / deceptive synthetic media / impersonation — industry-wide instaban.**
5. **Upwork day-1 housekeeping:** Account Settings → AI Preferences → **opt OUT of Work Product + Communications training** (you're opted in by default as of Jan 5, 2026) for client confidentiality. [source: terms.law; support.upwork.com]
6. **Two real humans, two real ID-verified profiles** (Marcel + Mike). One person faking two = ban. This is your legit 2x-shots-on-goal edge — use it.
7. **Off-platform contact to dodge fees = permanent suspension** on marketplaces. Keep marketplace work on-platform until the relationship migrates legitimately.
