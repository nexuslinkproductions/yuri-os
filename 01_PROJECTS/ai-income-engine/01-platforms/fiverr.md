Buyers search/browse, the algorithm ranks your gig, they message you or click Order. You wait to be found. This is why they "only see people offering services" — that IS the model. ~90% of new-seller income comes through this channel.

2) BRIEFS (the renamed/replaced buyer-request system). The old "Buyer Requests" board — where sellers could scroll a public feed of buyer needs and fire off proposals — was killed in 2023 and replaced by "Briefs" (now sometimes surfaced as "personalized offers" / "post a project") [source: help.fiverr.com; fiverrtutorials.com]. Critically: you do NOT browse Briefs and apply to whatever you want. Fiverr's AI matches a buyer's structured project description to a SMALL set of sellers (typically 3-5) whose gigs and history fit, then pushes that Brief to those sellers' inboxes. You then have 72 hours to "Create an offer" or "Ask questions." [source: help.fiverr.com]. Eligibility gate: Fiverr only routes Briefs to gigs with a Success Score of 7+ — meaning new sellers effectively cannot receive Briefs until they have an order history. So the one feature that looks like "applying to jobs" is gated behind exactly the reputation a new account does not have.

3) FIVERR PRO INVITES + PROMOTED GIGS (paid ad placement once eligible).

Net: a brand-new account in week 1 has NO way to proactively apply to posted needs. You list gigs and wait, or you drive your own external traffic. The "apply to jobs" mental model belongs to Upwork/bid boards, not Fiverr.

## Fiverr in 2026: The Real Mechanics for a 2-Person AI Unit

### 1. The mental-model fix: Fiverr is a storefront, not a job board
The operators' confusion is the single most important thing to clear up. On Fiverr you do not see "jobs you apply for" because Fiverr is a **gig marketplace** — sellers publish productized service listings ("Gigs"), buyers search and order. The old public feed where sellers scrolled buyer needs and fired off proposals ("Buyer Requests") was **retired in 2023** and replaced by **Briefs** [source: fiverrtutorials.com; help.fiverr.com]. Briefs are AI-matched and pushed to ~3-5 sellers' inboxes; you do not browse-and-bid, and you only receive them once a gig hits **Success Score 7+** — i.e. after you already have a track record. So in week 1 there is literally no "apply to posted jobs" surface. If the unit wants a true apply-to-jobs flow, that's Upwork, not Fiverr.

### 2. The levels ladder (this is your real roadmap)
Four tiers, evaluated against six metrics, promotions processed around the 15th of the month [source: eduearnhub.com; help.fiverr.com]:
- **New Seller** (day 1): near-zero visibility, no Briefs.
- **Level 1**: Success Score 5+, 4.7+ rating, 90% response rate, 10 orders, $500 earned.
- **Level 2**: Success Score 7, 4.6+ rating, 20 orders, 10 unique clients, $2,000 earned. **This unlocks Briefs** — the inbound pipeline. This is the goal of the 4-week sprint.
- **Top Rated**: Success Score 9, 40 orders, 20 unique clients, $10,000 earned, manual review.

The **Success Score (1-10 per gig)** is computed from order completion rate, on-time delivery, communication quality (sub-1hr responses), order quality, revision/dispute handling, and client satisfaction/value-for-money. Cancellations — even mutual — hurt it. Protect it like your life depends on it, because Briefs eligibility does.

### 3. The cold-start trap and how to beat it
Conversion rate is the dominant ranking signal, and a fresh account has none, so Fiverr won't feed it traffic. The algorithm favors keywords early (so SEO your gig titles/tags hard) but shifts to performance signals as orders accrue [source: zenlance.net]. The proven escape: **break the loop manually**. Bring 1-3 orders from your own network or external traffic in week 1, deliver overnight, bank 5-star reviews, and the conversion-rate signal flips on. Treat the first order as disproportionately valuable. Do NOT thrash your gig — every edit costs the algorithm 2-4 weeks of re-evaluation.

### 4. What actually sells (and where this unit has unfair advantage)
2026 demand has shifted from "I'll write a ChatGPT prompt" (now baseline, worthless) to **AI-native, high-ticket, end-to-end work**. Fiverr is deliberately steering the marketplace toward $500+ "AI-native" projects [source: gologin.com]. Highest-fit categories with real prices:
- **AI automation / agents** (n8n, Make, Zapier, VAPI voice): live gigs at $50-$80 entry, $75-$520 typical, retainers $300-$2,000/mo [source: fiverr.com/resources/guides; freelanceautomationai.com].
- **RAG chatbots / custom assistants** wired to a client's CRM/docs/API: $75-$1,500/project.
- **AI video generation & editing**: +329% YoY, fastest-growing skill on the market [source: medium.com/the-ai-studio]. **This is Marcel's home turf** — pairing real post-production/motion expertise with AI generation is a defensible, ToS-clean, premium offer few competitors can match.
- **AI web apps / SaaS MVPs / landing pages**: $200-$2,000.
- **AI content for SaaS/B2B** (human-edited): retainer territory.

The unit's edge: most Fiverr "AI" sellers ship thin raw output. A high-skill operator + Claude/Codex can deliver genuinely engineered, tested, integrated work overnight — which is exactly what Fiverr's algorithm and its higher-budget buyers now reward.

### 5. Pricing strategy
- **Week 1 loss-leader tier**: $25-$75 fixed packages, fast delivery, purely to win the first 3-5 orders and reviews. Accept the thin margin; you're buying ranking data.
- **Anchor with 3-tier packages** (Basic/Standard/Premium). Make the middle tier the obvious value buy; the Premium tier exists to anchor price and occasionally lands a whale.
- **Climb fast**: once you have ~5 reviews, raise prices toward category norms ($80-$250 AOV) and push the conversation toward **retainers** — that's where the real money and Success Score stability live.
- **Mind the math**: 20% commission + the buyer's 5.5% fee + $3 under-$100 fee means low tickets are punished. Price above $100 wherever defensible.
