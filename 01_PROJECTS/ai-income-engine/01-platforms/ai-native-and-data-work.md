**How work is actually obtained:** This is not one platform — it is a category with two opposite economic logics, and conflating them is the trap.

TRACK 1 — "Get paid to DO AI tasks" (expert data / RLHF). Mercor, Outlier (Scale-owned), DataAnnotation.tech, Surge AI, Toloka. Mechanic: you create a profile, pass skill screenings / an AI-interview, then either (a) get auto-matched and emailed unsolicited project offers (Mercor's "Verified Expert" model — high score = inbound offers for weeks/months [source: mercor.com, remowork.life]) or (b) pick up tasks from a project queue (Outlier/DataAnnotation). You are NOT applying to a buyer's posted job in a bid-board sense; you are screened into a contractor pool that assigns you work. Pay weekly via Stripe. CRITICAL: these platforms forbid using AI to generate answers. Mercor's LLM-usage policy permits only grammar/diction polish and subtle rephrasing 'provided the fundamental ideas and structure remain your own,' and explicitly prohibits using AI for assessing model responses, writing justifications, or predicting code behavior; 'submissions evidently produced by AI will be flagged' [source: talent.docs.mercor.com]. DataAnnotation/Outlier detect AI text using the very models you evaluate, and bans are permanent and often appeal-less [source: icytales.com, thegrounded.ghost.io].

## AI-Native & Data-Work Platforms (2026): The Two-Track Split That Decides Everything

The single most important thing to understand before spending one minute here: this category contains two opposite economic logics that look similar and pay completely differently for *you specifically*.

**Track 1 — get paid to DO AI tasks (expert data / RLHF).** Mercor, Outlier (Scale), DataAnnotation, Surge AI, Toloka. These platforms pay humans to train and evaluate models. Pay is real and sometimes high — Mercor averages ~$85/hr across 30k contractors, software/data-science contracts $50-150+/hr; Surge pays approved 'Surgers' ~$0.30-0.40/working-minute and tops out at $250-450/hr for medical fellows, $500-1,000/hr for VC partners on domain evals [source: mercor.com, surgehq.ai/sacra.com]. Outlier's expert tier is $30-50+/hr, legal up to $100/hr, chemistry $60/hr [source: aiworkfinder.com, feedkin.com].

**The catch that kills the Claude-unit thesis on Track 1:** the product *is* human cognition. AI assistance is explicitly forbidden and actively hunted. Mercor's LLM policy allows only grammar/tone polish 'provided the fundamental ideas and structure remain your own' and prohibits using AI to assess model outputs, write justifications, or predict code behavior — 'submissions evidently produced by AI will be flagged' [source: talent.docs.mercor.com]. DataAnnotation/Outlier detect AI text *using the same models you're rating*, and bans are permanent, instant, and frequently un-appealable [source: icytales.com, thegrounded.ghost.io]. There's even a black market in rented EU accounts because the gatekeeping is real [source: algorithmwatch.org]. Translation: on Track 1, your $40k YURI stack is contraband. You can only sell Marcel's *genuine human hours* — capped by the clock, zero AI leverage.

**Track 2 — sell AI-BUILT deliverables.** Here the client *wants* the automation. This is where a high-skill 2-person Claude unit prints money. Sub-channels:
- **AI automation agency** (the engine): n8n/Make workflow builds, $3k-6k per build + $500-3,000/mo retainers. Five clients on $3-5k retainers = $15-25k/mo recurring at 60-80% gross margin (tool+API cost only $200-1k/client) [source: monetizebot.ai, arsum.com].
- **Productized AI services** on Fiverr/Upwork: chatbot builds $300-1,500 (local businesses pay for a 24/7 agent on Voiceflow/Botpress that actually solves problems), fixed-scope content systems ('10 SEO posts in 48h') where fulfillment cost approaches zero [source: medium.com/the-ai-studio].

- **Digital products** on Gumroad: n8n workflow packs $29-299, prompt libraries, custom-GPT configs — solo creators do $500-1,500/mo, some $2,500/mo [source: digitalapplied.com, medium.com].

### What actually sells, and pricing strategy
Stop thinking 'hourly task.' Think **fixed-scope outcome with near-zero marginal cost.** The winning frame in 2026 is productized: a specific deliverable at a fixed price, fulfilled by your AI stack. Price on value, not effort. A $1,200 chatbot that saves a dental office 15 hours/week is a bargain to them and ~2 hours of your real work. Land the client on a build fee, convert to retainer (the recurring margin is the whole game). Stack a Gumroad catalog underneath for compounding passive income while you sleep.
