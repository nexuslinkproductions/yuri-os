**How work is actually obtained:** This cluster splits into three different "how you get work" mechanics, and conflating them is the #1 mistake.

TOPTAL = vetted-talent, NO open job board. You do NOT apply to posted jobs. You apply to JOIN the network (~3% accept, 3-8 week gauntlet: English/comms screen -> timed technical/skills test -> 1-3 week paid-ish test project -> final interview). Once in, Toptal MATCHERS hand-pick you and pitch you to clients. You interview with the client, not bid against a crowd. [source: earnifyhub.com, toptal.com/top-3-percent]

CONTRA = hybrid. Two paths: (1) Discover/profile — clients browse and DM you (productized, inbound). (2) Opportunities tab — a real job board where clients post projects and you APPLY/send proposals daily. So yes, you can apply to posted jobs on Contra. [source: contra.com/features/find-freelance-jobs]

BRAINTRUST = apply-to-matched-jobs. After signup + profile, your homepage shows matched roles. You click Apply, set hourly rate, answer screening questions, attach resume. BUT your applications are NOT shown to clients until you win your FIRST job and pass screening to earn the "Approved Talent" badge — a chicken-and-egg gate. [source: support.usebraintrust.com Guide: Becoming Approved Talent]

GUN.IO = vetted-talent, matching-only, NO applying. You complete a 100% profile + pass algorithmic screen + work-history review + live senior-engineer technical interview. Then Gun.io's team shortlists YOU for client roles (~100 hired/month of ~1,000 applicants). You never browse or bid on jobs. [source: gun.io/find-work, gun.io/faq]

WELLFOUND (ex-AngelList Talent) = pure job-board-apply. 70k+ open eng roles, one-click apply, free for seekers. Mostly FULL-TIME startup roles with equity; contract/freelance is a filter but a minority of listings. [source: wellfound.com, purshology.com]

## Premium & No-Fee Platforms: Toptal / Contra / Braintrust / Gun.io / Wellfound — 2026 Deep-Dive

### The mental model you're missing
You were confused by Fiverr because it only shows people *offering* gigs. This cluster fixes that — but in three different ways, and you must not blur them:

- **Apply-to-join networks (Toptal, Gun.io):** You don't apply to jobs. You apply to be *let in*. Then humans match you to clients. No bidding wars.
- **Apply-to-jobs platforms (Contra Opportunities tab, Braintrust matched roles, Wellfound):** Real job boards where you send proposals/applications. This is the "apply to posted jobs" model you were looking for.
- **Get-discovered profiles (Contra Discover):** Clients browse and DM you. Inbound, productized.

Contra is the only one that does *both* board + profile, which is why it's the realistic month-1 cash channel.

### Platform-by-platform reality

**TOPTAL — the premium prize, the slow gate.** ~3% acceptance, 3-8 week vetting (English/comms -> timed technical/skills test -> 1-3 week test project -> final interview) [source: earnifyhub.com, toptal.com/top-3-percent]. AI/ML and senior dev rates run **$150-250/hr**, AI integration + full-stack often **$200+/hr** [source: hireinsouth.com, secondtalent.com]. One contested point: some sources claim Toptal pockets ~50% of the client rate; others say the freelancer sets and keeps their rate. Treat the 50% figure as *folklore, not verified* — flag it, don't repeat it as fact [source: teilurtalent.com vs gun.io comparison; knowledge ~2025-2026]. **Not a 4-week money platform** — the sprint window is just the vetting window. It's a month-2-to-3 income engine you start *now*.

**GUN.IO — Toptal for engineers, matching-only.** ~10% accept (~100/1,000 monthly), 100% profile + algorithmic screen + work-history review + live senior-engineer interview [source: gun.io/faq, betterteam.com]. Rates **$100-200/hr**, freelancer keeps their set rate, fee charged to client [source: gun.io/find-work]. You never bid — they shortlist you. Same timing problem as Toptal: no month-1 cash, premium month-3 payoff. Best fit if the unit positions as a senior AI/full-stack engineering pair.

**BRAINTRUST — no-fee, but a cold-start trap.** Decentralized, no talent fees, client pays ~10-15% markup [source: usebraintrust.com, coinmarketcap.com]. You apply to matched roles (set rate, answer questions, attach resume) — *but your applications stay invisible to clients until you win your first job and earn the "Approved Talent" badge* [source: support.usebraintrust.com]. Multiple 2026 reviews report 3+ months with zero interviews [source: glassdoor.com, flexiple.com]. Ignore the BTRST token noise — it's governance/rewards, not your paycheck. **Lowest priority. Set up, then forget.**

**CONTRA — your actual week-1 weapon.** Commission-free for freelancers — keep 100% [source: contra.com/commission-free]. Client pays a transaction fee *capped at $29* [source: client-help.contra.com]. Contra Pro is an optional $29/mo for branding/analytics — skip it initially. Two work paths: **Discover** (clients browse profiles) + **Opportunities** (daily posted jobs you apply to) [source: contra.com/features/find-freelance-jobs]. Skews creative/design/AI-design — *exactly Marcel's lane* (motion, video, post, web). Honest caveat: lower job volume than Upwork; you'll need outbound DMs in the first 90 days [source: remote100k.com, earnifyhub.com]. Real case: a designer landed two $4k projects in month one [source: earnifyhub.com]. That's the achievable shape.

**WELLFOUND — adjacent, not core.** Free, one-click apply, 70k+ eng roles [source: wellfound.com], but it's a *full-time startup job board*. Contract/freelance exists as a filter but is the minority. Useful for landing a recurring contract or a fractional gig with a funded startup; not a fast-cash freelance marketplace.
