## The one-line answer to the Fiverr riddle
**Fiverr is a gig marketplace, not a job board — you cannot apply to posted jobs.** You list "Gigs" and wait to be found. The old "Buyer Requests" board was killed in 2023 and replaced by **Briefs**, which Fiverr's AI pushes only to 3-5 matched sellers — and only to accounts with a Success Score 7+ (a new account can't have one). **The "apply to jobs" model you were looking for is Upwork.**
