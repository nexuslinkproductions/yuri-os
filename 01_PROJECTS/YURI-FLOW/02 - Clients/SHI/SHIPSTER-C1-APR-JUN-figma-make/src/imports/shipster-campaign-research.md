# SHIPSTER Campaign: Target Group Research & Feasibility Analysis
## Executive Summary
SHIPSTER's addressable market is estimated at ~5,000–12,000 highly qualified prospects in the Swiss German-speaking regions where SHIPSTER operates. Acquiring 30 new clients (from 70 → 100) in 3 months is realistic but requires a well-funded, precision-targeted campaign with an estimated media budget of CHF 6,000–18,000 depending on conversion efficiency.
***
## The Swiss Boating Market
Switzerland has approximately 96,000 registered sport and leisure boats, of which roughly two-thirds (~64,000) are motorboats. The total boat count has been stable to slightly declining (−0.7% YoY in 2023), with motorboats holding steady while sailing boats decline. The highest concentrations are in cantons Waadt (15,588), Bern (11,650), Zürich (10,701), and Thurgau (8,001).[1][2][3]
The average boat owner is 60 years old, with the bulk of ownership in the 40–70 age range. This demographic skews older and wealthier, but the new entrant rate is slightly declining — a signal that younger professionals increasingly seek alternatives to ownership.[4]
***
## Kat. A License Holders in Switzerland
There is no centralized Swiss statistic for total Kat. A license holders. However, we can triangulate:
- ~64,000 registered private motorboats all require a Kat. A holder[2]
- Many license holders don't currently own a boat (lapsed owners, waitlisters, new graduates)
- Uri canton alone processed 30 boat license exams in 2023; scaling nationally, an estimated 2,000–3,500 new Kat. A licenses are issued per year[5]
- Since licenses don't expire, the cumulative pool of active Kat. A holders is estimated at 80,000–120,000 people nationwide
This is SHIPSTER's total license-qualified universe.

## Target Group Sizing & Funnel
SHIPSTER's ideal customer sits at the intersection of four filters: Kat. A license, sufficient income, no current boat/mooring, and proximity to SHIPSTER locations (Zürich, Jona, Stäfa, Beckenried, Arth).[6]
| Filter | Estimated Pool | % of Previous |
|---|---|---|
| Swiss adults (18+) | ~7.1 million | — |
| Kat. A license holders | ~100,000 | 1.4% |
| Income-qualified (top 35%) | ~60,000 | 60% |
| No boat or no mooring spot | ~25,000 | 42% |
| In SHIPSTER lake regions | ~12,000 | 48% |
| Reachable via digital ads | ~5,000 | 42% |
### Income Qualification
At CHF 3,990/year (~CHF 333/month), SHIPSTER requires households with meaningful discretionary income. The median Swiss gross household income is ~CHF 10,500/month. The top 20% earn over CHF 8,508 (single) or CHF 17,867 (family of four). Average monthly savings are CHF 1,546. A CHF 333/month commitment is comfortable for roughly the top 30–35% of households, representing about 60,000 of the Kat. A holder pool.[7][8][9]
### The Mooring Gap — SHIPSTER's Core Opportunity
This is the single most powerful market driver for SHIPSTER. Boat mooring spots on Swiss lakes are severely scarce:
- Zürich city: 2,500 spots with ~2,000 people on the waiting list; wait times range from 1 to over 20 years[10]
- Lake Zurich broadly: reports of 15–35 year waits[11][12]
- Genf: "fast unmöglich" to get a spot[13]
- The BAV confirms: available mooring spots at most lakes are fully utilized[1]
This means thousands of license holders who want to be on the water but cannot secure boat parking — exactly the audience SHIPSTER serves.

- Premium boats: Ganz Ovation 6.8 with 280–300 PS, 8 persons, waterski-capable[6]
- Sailbox/SailCom serve a different segment (sailing, Kat. D), not direct competitors
- Click&Boat/SamBoat are per-day rentals at CHF 350–500/day — SHIPSTER offers far better value for regular users

## Can You Reach 30 New Clients in 3 Months?
Yes — this is achievable. Here's the math:
### Scenario Modeling
For a niche, high-intent B2C product in Switzerland:
- Facebook/Instagram CPM Switzerland: ~$8.40 (CHF ~7.50), with Swiss ads running 30–50% above German benchmarks[15][16]
- Niche targeting (boating interest + income) will increase CPM to an estimated CHF 12–20
- Expected funnel: Impression → Click → Website → Lead → Conversion
- Conservative estimate: 1–2% lead-to-client conversion for a CHF 3,990 annual commitment
- CPL (cost per lead): CHF 30–80 for a well-targeted Swiss niche campaign
| Scenario | CPL | Conv. Rate | Leads Needed | Media Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Optimistic | CHF 30 | 2.0% | 1,500 | CHF 6,000 |
| Realistic | CHF 50 | 1.5% | 2,000 | CHF 10,000 |
| Conservative | CHF 80 | 1.0% | 3,000 | CHF 18,000 |
### Recommended Budget Allocation (3 Months)
- Total recommended media spend: CHF 10,000–15,000
- Creative production: Already covered by the campaign plan
- Agency/management (if outsourced): CHF 3,000–6,000
### Channel Mix Recommendation
- Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook): 60% of budget — best for visual storytelling, 9:16 format, interest-based targeting (boating, watersports, luxury lifestyle, Swiss lake regions)
- Google Search: 20% of budget — capture high-intent queries like "Motorboot mieten Zürichsee," "Boot sharing Schweiz," "Bootsplatz Warteliste Alternative"
- YouTube Pre-Roll: 10% of budget — leverage the Hero video (15s) as pre-roll for boating content
- Retargeting: 10% of budget — re-engage website visitors and app page visitors
***
## Key Targeting Parameters
### Primary Audience (Meta Ads)
- Age: 30–60 (core: 35–55)
- Location: Cantons ZH, SG, SZ, NW, LU + surrounding areas within 30 min of SHIPSTER locations
- Interests: Motorboating, watersports, wakeboarding, Swiss lakes, luxury lifestyle, boat brands
- Income proxy: Interests in premium brands, business travelers, property owners
- Exclusion: Current boat owners (exclude via boat brand purchase behavior)
### Secondary Audience (Lookalike)
- 1% Lookalike of current 70 SHIPSTER clients (if email list available for custom audience upload)
- Lookalike of website visitors (requires Pixel data from past season)
### Partnership Channels (Organic/Low-Cost)
- Boat license schools: Partner with Bootsfahrschulen to reach fresh Kat. A graduates
- Marina waiting lists: Advertise directly to people stuck on mooring waiting lists (leaflets, partnerships)
- Yacht clubs & sailing clubs: Cross-sell to Kat. A holders in club memberships
- Referral program: Offer current 70 clients credits for each successful referral
***
## Risk Factors & Mitigation
- Seasonality: The campaign launches March–May, perfectly timed as demand peaks before summer. The urgency message ("Dein Sommer") lands strongest in April–May.
- Niche audience: Small pool requires precise targeting to avoid ad fatigue. Rotate creatives across all 10 videos to maintain freshness.
- CHF 3,990 price point: Not an impulse buy. Expect a 2–4 week consideration period. Retargeting and email nurturing are essential.
- Competition from Mobox: Sailbox's new Mobox motorboat sharing is a nascent threat but currently has only 1 boat at 1 location  — SHIPSTER's fleet and multi-lake coverage is a clear advantage.[17]
***
## Bottom Line
Switzerland has an estimated 100,000 Kat. A license holders, of which roughly 12,000 are in SHIPSTER's serviceable regions with the right income profile and no current boat/mooring solution. With ~5,000 reachable via digital channels and a well-executed 3-month campaign at CHF 10,000–15,000 media spend, acquiring 30 new clients is a realistic target — representing a 0.6% conversion of the digitally reachable pool. Combined with referral programs and boat school partnerships, the path from 70 to 100+ clients by end of summer is achievable.
